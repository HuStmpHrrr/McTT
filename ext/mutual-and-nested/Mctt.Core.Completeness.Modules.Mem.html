Term Members of Valid Units

A term member a valid unit, or a module expression valid in what it denotes, has by lookup is valid at the type lookup reads, its δ-reduct at it too (valid_unit_mem_all). A definition of a body, read through the entries its local opens generate, is related to the definition of the same name of any body the module PER relates to it (body_sel_term); a unit lacking parameters types its members at the Π over them (unit_walk).
