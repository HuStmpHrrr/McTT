Freshness and Un-weakening

A variable that does not occur can be dropped. exp_fresh k M says that #k does not occur free in M, and unwk k is the renaming that drops index k; together they invert q^k ↑, the weakening that inserts a variable at position k:
  • exp_unwk_wk: dropping what was just inserted gives the term back, with no hypothesis;
  • exp_unwk_spec: inserting what was dropped gives the term back, when the dropped variable did not occur;
  • exp_wk_fresh: what q^k ↑ produces never mentions #k.
Nothing here is a judgment: these are facts about terms and renamings. They are what the checker needs to turn a level that is typed in an extended context, and does not mention the new variable, into a level of the shorter one. The same three facts are proved for normal and neutral forms, where the un-weakening has to produce a normal form again (nf_unwk) rather than go through nf_to_exp.

Un-weakening

unwk k fixes the indices below k, sends k to itself (any value would do: a fresh term never looks at it) and decrements those above. It is the renaming q^k pred, so it acts on terms by exp_wk and needs no new recursion.
Definition unwk (k : nat) : wk := wk_qn k pred.

#k Does Not Occur

One clause per syntactic sort, as for exp_wk: under n binders the index to avoid is n + k. A telescope shifts by its own length, and a module body by its binders, exactly as the weakenings do.
Fixpoint exp_fresh (k : nat) (M : exp) {struct M} : Prop :=
  match M with
  | a_typ _ | a_level _ | a_llit _
  | a_True | a_true | a_False => True
  | a_univ M | a_succl M => exp_fresh k M
  | a_maxl M N | a_app M N => exp_fresh k M /\ exp_fresh k N
  | a_exfalso A M => exp_fresh (S k) A /\ exp_fresh k M
  | a_ind _ _ | a_con _ _ _ | a_nlit _ | a_ntok => True
  | a_elim _ Us _ Bks M =>
      all_list (exp_fresh k) Us /\
      all_blocks (fun nm oZ A Bs => opt_all (exp_fresh (nm - 1 + k)) oZ /\ exp_fresh (nm + k) A /\
                                    all_brs (fun n B => exp_fresh (n + k) B) Bs) Bks /\ exp_fresh k M
  | a_pi A B | a_fn A B => exp_fresh k A /\ exp_fresh (S k) B
  | a_var x => x <> k
  | a_let b B => bnd_fresh k b /\ exp_fresh (S k) B
  | a_mem H _ => modexp_fresh k H
  end
with modexp_fresh (k : nat) (H : modexp) {struct H} : Prop :=
  match H with
  | me_unit _ => True
  | me_var x => x <> k
  | me_mem H _ => modexp_fresh k H
  | me_app H N => modexp_fresh k H /\ exp_fresh k N
  | me_lit U => gunit_fresh k U
  end
with bnd_fresh (k : nat) (b : bnd) {struct b} : Prop :=
  match b with
  | b_def oA M => match oA with Some A => exp_fresh k A | None => True end /\ exp_fresh k M
  | b_mod U => gunit_fresh k U
  end
with gunit_fresh (k : nat) (U : gunit) {struct U} : Prop :=
  match U with
  | gu_mk Δ D =>
      (fix tele_fresh (Δ : list centry) : Prop :=
         match Δ with
         | nil => True
         | cons e Δ' => centry_fresh (List.length Δ' + k) e /\ tele_fresh Δ'
         end) Δ
      /\ moddef_fresh (List.length Δ + k) D
  end
with moddef_fresh (k : nat) (D : moddef) {struct D} : Prop :=
  match D with
  | md_body sm Φ => gmod_fresh k Φ
  | md_alias E => modexp_fresh k E
  end
with gmod_fresh (k : nat) (Φ : gmod) {struct Φ} : Prop :=
  match Φ with
  | gm_nil => True
  | gm_ext Φ _ E => gmod_fresh k Φ /\ gentry_fresh (gm_binders Φ + k) E
  | gm_open Φ H _ => gmod_fresh k Φ /\ modexp_fresh (gm_binders Φ + k) H
  end
with gentry_fresh (k : nat) (E : gentry) {struct E} : Prop :=
  match E with
  | ge_def _ _ A B => exp_fresh k A /\ match B with Some M => exp_fresh k M | None => True end
  | ge_mod _ U => gunit_fresh k U
  | ge_ind _ _ _ D Φ => gmod_fresh (ind_nu D + k) Φ
  | ge_ind_alias _ _ _ _ _ _ => True
  end
with centry_fresh (k : nat) (e : centry) {struct e} : Prop :=
  match e with
  | ce_ass A => exp_fresh k A
  | ce_def A M => exp_fresh k A /\ exp_fresh k M
  | ce_mod U => gunit_fresh k U
  end.

The telescope clause of gunit_fresh, as a definition of its own.
Fixpoint tele_fresh (k : nat) (Δ : list centry) : Prop :=
  match Δ with
  | nil => True
  | cons e Δ' => centry_fresh (List.length Δ' + k) e /\ tele_fresh k Δ'
  end.

The blocks clause of exp_fresh, as a definition of its own.
Definition blocks_fresh (k : nat) (Bks : list eblock) : Prop :=
  all_blocks (fun nm oZ A Bs => opt_all (exp_fresh (nm - 1 + k)) oZ /\ exp_fresh (nm + k) A /\
                                all_brs (fun n B => exp_fresh (n + k) B) Bs) Bks.

Two Classes of Renaming

A renaming that is the identity away from k fixes every term in which #k does not occur; a renaming that never hits k produces only such terms. Both classes are closed under q and so under q^n, at the index the binders shift it to.
Definition wk_id_away (k : nat) (χ : wk) : Prop := forall x, x <> k -> χ x = x.

Lemma wk_id_away_q : forall k χ, wk_id_away k χ -> wk_id_away (S k) (wk_q χ).
Proof. intros k χ H [|x] Hx; cbn; [ reflexivity | rewrite H by lia; reflexivity ]. Qed.

Lemma wk_id_away_qn : forall n k χ, wk_id_away k χ -> wk_id_away (n + k) (wk_qn n χ).
Proof. induction n; intros; cbn; [ assumption | apply wk_id_away_q; auto ]. Qed.

Definition wk_avoids (k : nat) (φ : wk) : Prop := forall x, φ x <> k.

Lemma wk_avoids_q : forall k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> wk_avoids (S k) (wk_q φ).
Proof. intros k φ H [|x]; cbn; [ lia | specialize (H x); lia ]. Qed.

Lemma wk_avoids_qn : forall n k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> wk_avoids (n + k) (wk_qn n φ).
Proof. induction n; intros; cbn; [ assumption | apply wk_avoids_q; auto ]. Qed.

#[local]
Hint Resolve wk_id_away_q wk_id_away_qn wk_avoids_q wk_avoids_qn : core.

Lemma syn_fresh_id :
  (forall M k χ, exp_fresh k M -> wk_id_away k χ -> M[χ]ʷ = M) /\
  (forall H k χ, modexp_fresh k H -> wk_id_away k χ -> modexp_wk H χ = H) /\
  (forall b k χ, bnd_fresh k b -> wk_id_away k χ -> bnd_wk b χ = b) /\
  (forall U k χ, gunit_fresh k U -> wk_id_away k χ -> gunit_wk U χ = U) /\
  (forall D k χ, moddef_fresh k D -> wk_id_away k χ -> moddef_wk D χ = D) /\
  (forall Φ k χ, gmod_fresh k Φ -> wk_id_away k χ -> gmod_wk Φ χ = Φ) /\
  (forall E k χ, gentry_fresh k E -> wk_id_away k χ -> gentry_wk E χ = E) /\
  (forall e k χ, centry_fresh k e -> wk_id_away k χ -> centry_wk e χ = e).
Proof.
  syn_mut_ind; fresh_case;
    try solve [ match goal with H : wk_id_away _ _ |- _ => apply H; assumption end
              | match goal with H : forall A, Some ?X = Some A -> _ |- _ => eapply (H X); eauto end ].

Lemma syn_wk_fresh :
  (forall M k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> exp_fresh k M[φ]ʷ) /\
  (forall H k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> modexp_fresh k (modexp_wk H φ)) /\
  (forall b k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> bnd_fresh k (bnd_wk b φ)) /\
  (forall U k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> gunit_fresh k (gunit_wk U φ)) /\
  (forall D k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> moddef_fresh k (moddef_wk D φ)) /\
  (forall Φ k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> gmod_fresh k (gmod_wk Φ φ)) /\
  (forall E k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> gentry_fresh k (gentry_wk E φ)) /\
  (forall e k φ, wk_avoids k φ -> centry_fresh k (centry_wk e φ)).
Proof.
  syn_mut_ind; fresh_case; rewrite ?gm_binders_wk, ?length_tele_wk; eauto;
    try solve [ match goal with H : forall A, Some ?X = Some A -> _ |- _ => eapply (H X); eauto end ].
  all: try ups_case ltac:(fun H => eapply H; eassumption).
  1: blocks_case ltac:(fun H => eapply H; auto).
  match goal with
  | HF : List.Forall _ _ |- _ =>
      clear - HF H1; induction HF; cbn in *; [ exact I | rewrite length_tele_wk; split; eauto ]
  end.
Qed.

A renaming that sends only k to k' keeps #k' out of what kept #k out.
Definition wk_reflects (k k' : nat) (φ : wk) : Prop := forall x, φ x = k' -> x = k.

Lemma wk_reflects_q : forall k k' φ, wk_reflects k k' φ -> wk_reflects (S k) (S k') (wk_q φ).
Proof. intros k k' φ H [|x] Hx; cbn in Hx; [ discriminate | injection Hx as Hx; rewrite (H _ Hx); reflexivity ]. Qed.

Lemma wk_reflects_qn : forall n k k' φ, wk_reflects k k' φ -> wk_reflects (n + k) (n + k') (wk_qn n φ).
Proof. induction n; intros; cbn; [ assumption | apply wk_reflects_q; auto ]. Qed.

#[local]
Hint Resolve wk_reflects_q wk_reflects_qn : core.

Lemma syn_fresh_wk :
  (forall M k k' φ, exp_fresh k M -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> exp_fresh k' M[φ]ʷ) /\
  (forall H k k' φ, modexp_fresh k H -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> modexp_fresh k' (modexp_wk H φ)) /\
  (forall b k k' φ, bnd_fresh k b -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> bnd_fresh k' (bnd_wk b φ)) /\
  (forall U k k' φ, gunit_fresh k U -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> gunit_fresh k' (gunit_wk U φ)) /\
  (forall D k k' φ, moddef_fresh k D -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> moddef_fresh k' (moddef_wk D φ)) /\
  (forall Φ k k' φ, gmod_fresh k Φ -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> gmod_fresh k' (gmod_wk Φ φ)) /\
  (forall E k k' φ, gentry_fresh k E -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> gentry_fresh k' (gentry_wk E φ)) /\
  (forall e k k' φ, centry_fresh k e -> wk_reflects k k' φ -> centry_fresh k' (centry_wk e φ)).
Proof.
  syn_mut_ind; fresh_case; rewrite ?gm_binders_wk, ?length_tele_wk; eauto;
    try solve [ match goal with H : wk_reflects _ _ _ |- _ => intros Heq; apply H in Heq; contradiction end
              | match goal with H : forall A, Some ?X = Some A -> _ |- _ => eapply (H X); eauto end ].
  all: try ups_case ltac:(fun H => eapply H; eassumption).
  - blocks_case ltac:(fun H => eapply H; [ eassumption | apply wk_reflects_qn; assumption ]).
  - match goal with
    | HF : List.Forall _ ?Δ, Hf : _ ?Δ, Hw : wk_reflects _ _ _ |- _ =>
        clear - HF Hf Hw; induction HF as [| ? ? HB ? IH]; cbn in *; destruct_conjs; [ exact I | rewrite length_tele_wk; split ];
        [ eapply HB; [ eassumption | apply wk_reflects_qn; assumption ] | apply IH; assumption ]
    end.
Qed.

Corollary modexp_fresh_shiftn : forall H k n, modexp_fresh k H -> modexp_fresh (n + k) (modexp_wk H (wk_shiftn n)).
Proof.
  intros * Hf; eapply (proj1 (proj2 syn_fresh_wk)); [ exact Hf |].
  intros x Hx; cbn in Hx; lia.
Qed.

Corollary gentry_fresh_shiftn : forall E k n, gentry_fresh k E -> gentry_fresh (n + k) (gentry_wk E (wk_shiftn n)).
Proof.
  intros * Hf; eapply (proj1 (proj2 (proj2 (proj2 (proj2 (proj2 (proj2 syn_fresh_wk))))))); [ exact Hf |].
  intros x Hx; cbn in Hx; lia.
Qed.

unwk k Inverts q^k ↑

One way round the composite is the identity away from k, the other is the identity outright.
Lemma unwk_after_wk : forall k, wk_id_away k (unwk k ⊙ wk_qn k ↑).
Proof.
  intros k x Hx; unfold unwk; cbn.
  destruct (Compare_dec.lt_dec x k).
  - rewrite (wk_qn_lt _ _ x) by lia; rewrite wk_qn_lt by lia; reflexivity.
  - rewrite (wk_qn_ge k pred x) by lia; cbn; rewrite wk_qn_ge by lia; cbn; lia.
Qed.

Lemma wk_after_unwk : forall k, wk_eq (wk_qn k ↑ ⊙ unwk k) wk_id.
Proof.
  intros k x; unfold unwk; cbn.
  destruct (Compare_dec.lt_dec x k).
  - rewrite (wk_qn_lt _ _ x) by lia; rewrite wk_qn_lt by lia; reflexivity.
  - rewrite (wk_qn_ge k ↑ x) by lia; cbv [wk_shift]; rewrite wk_qn_ge by lia;
      rewrite Nat.add_sub; cbn [pred]; lia.
Qed.

Lemma wk_qn_shift_avoids : forall k, wk_avoids k (wk_qn k ↑).
Proof. intros k; rewrite <- (Nat.add_0_r k) at 1; apply wk_avoids_qn; intros x; cbn; lia. Qed.

Theorem exp_unwk_spec : forall k M, exp_fresh k M -> M = M[unwk k]ʷ[wk_qn k ↑]ʷ.
Proof.
  intros; rewrite (exp_wk_wk_ext _ _ _ (unwk k ⊙ wk_qn k ↑)) by reflexivity.
  symmetry; eapply (proj1 syn_fresh_id); eauto using unwk_after_wk.
Qed.

Theorem exp_unwk_wk : forall k M, M[wk_qn k ↑]ʷ[unwk k]ʷ = M.
Proof. intros; rewrite (exp_wk_wk_ext _ _ _ wk_id) by apply wk_after_unwk; apply exp_wk_id. Qed.

Theorem exp_wk_fresh : forall k M, exp_fresh k M[wk_qn k ↑]ʷ.
Proof. intros; apply (proj1 syn_wk_fresh), wk_qn_shift_avoids. Qed.

Corollary exp_shift_fresh : forall M, exp_fresh 0 M[↑]ʷ.
Proof. intros; exact (exp_wk_fresh 0 M). Qed.

Normal and Neutral Forms

The checker drops a variable from a *normal form* and must get a normal form back, so freshness and un-weakening are defined again on nf, ne and the atoms of a canonical level, and related to the versions on terms through nf_to_exp.
nf_unwk is a renaming of neutrals, so it preserves the shape of a normal form; it does **not** preserve canonicity of a level, since dropping a variable changes the order of the atoms. The checker therefore re-canonicalises (lvl_canon) after un-weakening a level. The induction principle on normal forms, the six types at once.
Section Nf_Induction.
  Variables (P : nf -> Prop) (P0 : ne -> Prop) (P1 : lvl_atoms -> Prop) (P2 : nfs -> Prop)
    (P3 : nbrs -> Prop) (P4 : nblocks -> Prop).
  Hypotheses
    (f_typ : forall i, P (nf_typ i))
    (f_univ : forall c xs, P1 xs -> P (nf_univ c xs))
    (f_level : forall n, P (nf_level n))
    (f_lvl : forall c xs, P1 xs -> P (nf_lvl c xs))
    (f_True : P nf_True)
    (f_true : P nf_true)
    (f_False : P nf_False)
    (f_pi : forall A, P A -> forall B, P B -> P (nf_pi A B))
    (f_fn : forall A, P A -> forall M, P M -> P (nf_fn A M))
    (f_indty : forall p k Ms, P2 Ms -> P (nf_indty p k Ms))
    (f_con : forall p k c Ms, P2 Ms -> P (nf_con p k c Ms))
    (f_neut : forall u, P0 u -> P (nf_neut u))
    (f_exfalso : forall A, P A -> forall u, P0 u -> P0 (ne_exfalso A u))
    (f_elim : forall p Us, P2 Us -> forall m Bks, P4 Bks -> forall u, P0 u -> P0 (ne_elim p Us m Bks u))
    (f_app : forall u, P0 u -> forall W, P W -> P0 (ne_app u W))
    (f_var : forall x, P0 (ne_var x))
    (f_glob : forall p, P0 (ne_glob p))
    (f_la_nil : P1 la_nil)
    (f_la_cons : forall j s u, P0 u -> forall r, P1 r -> P1 (la_cons j s u r))
    (f_nfs_nil : P2 nfs_nil)
    (f_nfs_cons : forall M, P M -> forall Ms, P2 Ms -> P2 (nfs_cons M Ms))
    (f_nbrs_nil : P3 nbrs_nil)
    (f_nbrs_cons : forall c n B, P B -> forall Bs, P3 Bs -> P3 (nbrs_cons c n B Bs))
    (f_nblocks_nil : P4 nblocks_nil)
    (f_nblocks_cons : forall nm A, P A -> forall Bs, P3 Bs -> forall Bks, P4 Bks -> P4 (nblocks_cons nm A Bs Bks)).

The mutual induction principle over normal forms and their sorts.
  Fixpoint nf_mind (W : nf) : P W :=
    match W with
    | nf_typ i => f_typ i
    | nf_univ c xs => f_univ c xs (la_mind xs)
    | nf_level n => f_level n
    | nf_lvl c xs => f_lvl c xs (la_mind xs)
    | nf_True => f_True
    | nf_true => f_true
    | nf_False => f_False
    | nf_pi A B => f_pi A (nf_mind A) B (nf_mind B)
    | nf_fn A M => f_fn A (nf_mind A) M (nf_mind M)
    | nf_indty p k Ms => f_indty p k Ms (nfs_mind Ms)
    | nf_con p k c Ms => f_con p k c Ms (nfs_mind Ms)
    | nf_neut u => f_neut u (ne_mind u)
    end
  with ne_mind (u : ne) : P0 u :=
    match u with
    | ne_exfalso A u => f_exfalso A (nf_mind A) u (ne_mind u)
    | ne_elim p Us m Bks u => f_elim p Us (nfs_mind Us) m Bks (nblocks_mind Bks) u (ne_mind u)
    | ne_app u W => f_app u (ne_mind u) W (nf_mind W)
    | ne_var x => f_var x
    | ne_glob p => f_glob p
    end
  with la_mind (xs : lvl_atoms) : P1 xs :=
    match xs with
    | la_nil => f_la_nil
    | la_cons j s u r => f_la_cons j s u (ne_mind u) r (la_mind r)
    end
  with nfs_mind (Ms : nfs) : P2 Ms :=
    match Ms with
    | nfs_nil => f_nfs_nil
    | nfs_cons M Ms => f_nfs_cons M (nf_mind M) Ms (nfs_mind Ms)
    end
  with nbrs_mind (Bs : nbrs) : P3 Bs :=
    match Bs with
    | nbrs_nil => f_nbrs_nil
    | nbrs_cons c n B Bs => f_nbrs_cons c n B (nf_mind B) Bs (nbrs_mind Bs)
    end
  with nblocks_mind (Bks : nblocks) : P4 Bks :=
    match Bks with
    | nblocks_nil => f_nblocks_nil
    | nblocks_cons nm A Bs Bks => f_nblocks_cons nm A (nf_mind A) Bs (nbrs_mind Bs) Bks (nblocks_mind Bks)
    end.

  Lemma nf_mut_ind :
    (forall W, P W) /\ (forall u, P0 u) /\ (forall xs, P1 xs) /\ (forall Ms, P2 Ms) /\
    (forall Bs, P3 Bs) /\ (forall Bks, P4 Bks).
  Proof. repeat split; [ exact nf_mind | exact ne_mind | exact la_mind | exact nfs_mind | exact nbrs_mind | exact nblocks_mind ]. Qed.
End Nf_Induction.

Freshness of an eliminator: of its uniform parameters, its blocks and its scrutinee.
Lemma exp_fresh_elim : forall k oq Us m Bks M,
    exp_fresh k (a_elim oq Us m Bks M) <->
      all_list (exp_fresh k) Us /\ blocks_fresh k Bks /\ exp_fresh k M.
Proof. reflexivity. Qed.

Lemma apps_exp_fresh : forall Ms M k,
    exp_fresh k M -> List.Forall (exp_fresh k) Ms -> exp_fresh k (apps_exp M Ms).
Proof. induction Ms; intros * HM HF; inversion HF; subst; cbn; [ assumption | apply IHMs; [ split |]; assumption ]. Qed.

nf_fresh k W: the variable k does not occur in the normal form W; mutually, for its other sorts.
Fixpoint nf_fresh (k : nat) (W : nf) {struct W} : Prop :=
  match W with
  | nf_typ _ | nf_level _ | nf_True | nf_true | nf_False => True
  | nf_univ _ xs | nf_lvl _ xs => la_fresh k xs
  | nf_pi A B | nf_fn A B => nf_fresh k A /\ nf_fresh (S k) B
  | nf_indty _ _ Ms | nf_con _ _ _ Ms => nfs_fresh k Ms
  | nf_neut u => ne_fresh k u
  end
with ne_fresh (k : nat) (u : ne) {struct u} : Prop :=
  match u with
  | ne_exfalso A u => nf_fresh (S k) A /\ ne_fresh k u
  | ne_elim _ Us _ Bks u => nfs_fresh k Us /\ nblocks_fresh k Bks /\ ne_fresh k u
  | ne_app u W => ne_fresh k u /\ nf_fresh k W
  | ne_var x => x <> k
  | ne_glob _ => True
  end
with la_fresh (k : nat) (xs : lvl_atoms) {struct xs} : Prop :=
  match xs with
  | la_nil => True
  | la_cons _ _ u r => ne_fresh k u /\ la_fresh k r
  end
with nfs_fresh (k : nat) (Ms : nfs) {struct Ms} : Prop :=
  match Ms with
  | nfs_nil => True
  | nfs_cons M Ms => nf_fresh k M /\ nfs_fresh k Ms
  end
with nbrs_fresh (k : nat) (Bs : nbrs) {struct Bs} : Prop :=
  match Bs with
  | nbrs_nil => True
  | nbrs_cons _ n B Bs => nf_fresh (n + k) B /\ nbrs_fresh k Bs
  end
with nblocks_fresh (k : nat) (Bks : nblocks) {struct Bks} : Prop :=
  match Bks with
  | nblocks_nil => True
  | nblocks_cons nm A Bs Bks => nf_fresh (nm + k) A /\ nbrs_fresh k Bs /\ nblocks_fresh k Bks
  end.

The normal form W with the variable k removed: the variables above it moved down by one; the inverse of the weakening at k when k is fresh in W.
Fixpoint nf_unwk (k : nat) (W : nf) {struct W} : nf :=
  match W with
  | nf_typ i => nf_typ i
  | nf_univ c xs => nf_univ c (la_unwk k xs)
  | nf_level n => nf_level n
  | nf_lvl c xs => nf_lvl c (la_unwk k xs)
  | nf_True => nf_True
  | nf_true => nf_true
  | nf_False => nf_False
  | nf_pi A B => nf_pi (nf_unwk k A) (nf_unwk (S k) B)
  | nf_fn A M => nf_fn (nf_unwk k A) (nf_unwk (S k) M)
  | nf_indty p j Ms => nf_indty p j (nfs_unwk k Ms)
  | nf_con p j c Ms => nf_con p j c (nfs_unwk k Ms)
  | nf_neut u => nf_neut (ne_unwk k u)
  end
with ne_unwk (k : nat) (u : ne) {struct u} : ne :=
  match u with
  | ne_exfalso A u => ne_exfalso (nf_unwk (S k) A) (ne_unwk k u)
  | ne_elim p Us m Bks u => ne_elim p (nfs_unwk k Us) m (nblocks_unwk k Bks) (ne_unwk k u)
  | ne_app u W => ne_app (ne_unwk k u) (nf_unwk k W)
  | ne_var x => ne_var (unwk k x)
  | ne_glob p => ne_glob p
  end
with la_unwk (k : nat) (xs : lvl_atoms) {struct xs} : lvl_atoms :=
  match xs with
  | la_nil => la_nil
  | la_cons j s u r => la_cons j s (ne_unwk k u) (la_unwk k r)
  end
with nfs_unwk (k : nat) (Ms : nfs) {struct Ms} : nfs :=
  match Ms with
  | nfs_nil => nfs_nil
  | nfs_cons M Ms => nfs_cons (nf_unwk k M) (nfs_unwk k Ms)
  end
with nbrs_unwk (k : nat) (Bs : nbrs) {struct Bs} : nbrs :=
  match Bs with
  | nbrs_nil => nbrs_nil
  | nbrs_cons c n B Bs => nbrs_cons c n (nf_unwk (n + k) B) (nbrs_unwk k Bs)
  end
with nblocks_unwk (k : nat) (Bks : nblocks) {struct Bks} : nblocks :=
  match Bks with
  | nblocks_nil => nblocks_nil
  | nblocks_cons nm A Bs Bks => nblocks_cons nm (nf_unwk (nm + k) A) (nbrs_unwk k Bs) (nblocks_unwk k Bks)
  end.

Freshness of a normal form is decidable: it is a conjunction of disequalities of indices, so it is decided by a boolean function and reflection.
Fixpoint nf_freshb (k : nat) (W : nf) {struct W} : bool :=
  match W with
  | nf_typ _ | nf_level _ | nf_True | nf_true | nf_False => true
  | nf_univ _ xs | nf_lvl _ xs => la_freshb k xs
  | nf_pi A B | nf_fn A B => nf_freshb k A && nf_freshb (S k) B
  | nf_indty _ _ Ms | nf_con _ _ _ Ms => nfs_freshb k Ms
  | nf_neut u => ne_freshb k u
  end
with ne_freshb (k : nat) (u : ne) {struct u} : bool :=
  match u with
  | ne_exfalso A u => nf_freshb (S k) A && ne_freshb k u
  | ne_elim _ Us _ Bks u => nfs_freshb k Us && nblocks_freshb k Bks && ne_freshb k u
  | ne_app u W => ne_freshb k u && nf_freshb k W
  | ne_var x => negb (Nat.eqb x k)
  | ne_glob _ => true
  end
with la_freshb (k : nat) (xs : lvl_atoms) {struct xs} : bool :=
  match xs with
  | la_nil => true
  | la_cons _ _ u r => ne_freshb k u && la_freshb k r
  end
with nfs_freshb (k : nat) (Ms : nfs) {struct Ms} : bool :=
  match Ms with
  | nfs_nil => true
  | nfs_cons M Ms => nf_freshb k M && nfs_freshb k Ms
  end
with nbrs_freshb (k : nat) (Bs : nbrs) {struct Bs} : bool :=
  match Bs with
  | nbrs_nil => true
  | nbrs_cons _ n B Bs => nf_freshb (n + k) B && nbrs_freshb k Bs
  end
with nblocks_freshb (k : nat) (Bks : nblocks) {struct Bks} : bool :=
  match Bks with
  | nblocks_nil => true
  | nblocks_cons nm A Bs Bks => nf_freshb (nm + k) A && nbrs_freshb k Bs && nblocks_freshb k Bks
  end.

Lemma nf_freshb_iff :
  (forall W k, nf_freshb k W = true <-> nf_fresh k W) /\
  (forall u k, ne_freshb k u = true <-> ne_fresh k u) /\
  (forall xs k, la_freshb k xs = true <-> la_fresh k xs) /\
  (forall Ms k, nfs_freshb k Ms = true <-> nfs_fresh k Ms) /\
  (forall Bs k, nbrs_freshb k Bs = true <-> nbrs_fresh k Bs) /\
  (forall Bks k, nblocks_freshb k Bks = true <-> nblocks_fresh k Bks).
Proof.
  apply nf_mut_ind; intros; cbn;
    repeat rewrite Bool.andb_true_iff;
    try solve [ split; [ intros _; exact I | reflexivity ]
              | rewrite Bool.negb_true_iff, Nat.eqb_neq; reflexivity
              | firstorder ].
Qed.

  destruct Hf as [Hb Hr]; destruct Hi as [(_ & _ & ->) | Hi]; [ exact Hb | eapply IH; eassumption ].
Qed.

Lemma lvl_le_la_fresh : forall k l l', lvl_le l l' -> la_fresh k (snd l') ->

The Bound of a Level Under a Binder

The least level of the context that is above a level l of the context extended by a variable, for every value of the variable: an atom fresh at k stays, un-weakened, and an atom that mentions k, of sort s, ranges over the ordinals below ω·(s+1), which is the bound it contributes (lvl_bound_le, lvl_le_bound).
Fixpoint la_fresh_part (k : nat) (xs : lvl_atoms) : lvl_atoms :=
  match xs with
  | la_nil => la_nil
  | la_cons j s a r => if ne_freshb k a then la_cons j s a (la_fresh_part k r) else la_fresh_part k r
  end.

Fixpoint la_open_bound (k : nat) (xs : lvl_atoms) : o2 :=
  match xs with
  | la_nil => oz
  | la_cons j s a r => if ne_freshb k a then la_open_bound k r else omax (S s, 0) (la_open_bound k r)
  end.

Definition lvl_bound (k : nat) (l : lvl) : lvl :=
  (omax (fst l) (la_open_bound k (snd l)), la_unwk k (la_fresh_part k (snd l))).

The bound, weakened back, is above the level: the fresh atoms are the same, and an atom of sort s is below ω·(s+1).
Lemma lvl_bound_above : forall k l,
    lvl_le l (omax (fst l) (la_open_bound k (snd l)), la_fresh_part k (snd l)).
Proof.
  intros k [c xs]; apply lvl_le_correct; intros ν Hν; unfold lvl_ev; cbn [fst snd].
  enough (ole (la_ev ν xs) (omax (la_open_bound k xs) (la_ev ν (la_fresh_part k xs)))) as H
    by (revert H; generalize (la_ev ν xs) (la_open_bound k xs) (la_ev ν (la_fresh_part k xs)); intros; ord).
  induction xs as [| j s a r IH]; cbn [la_ev la_open_bound la_fresh_part]; [ ord |].
  specialize (Hν a s).
  destruct (ne_freshb k a); cbn [la_ev].
  - revert IH; generalize (la_ev ν r) (la_open_bound k r) (la_ev ν (la_fresh_part k r)) (ν a s); intros; ord.
  - revert IH Hν; generalize (la_ev ν r) (la_open_bound k r) (la_ev ν (la_fresh_part k r)) (ν a s); intros; ord.
Qed.

The bound is below every level fresh at k above the level, un-weakened: so it is the least such level of the context.

Lemma la_open_bound_le : forall k xs d,
    (forall j s a, la_In j s a xs -> ~ ne_fresh k a -> s < fst d) ->
    ole (la_open_bound k xs) d.
Proof.
  intros k xs d; induction xs as [| j s a r IH]; cbn [la_open_bound]; intros H; [ apply ole_zero |].
  assert (IH' : ole (la_open_bound k r) d) by (apply IH; intros; eapply H; [ right |]; eassumption).
  destruct (ne_freshb k a) eqn:E; [ exact IH' |].
  assert (Hs : s < fst d).
  { eapply H; [ left; auto |]. intros Hf; apply (proj1 (proj2 nf_freshb_iff)) in Hf; congruence. }
  revert IH' Hs; generalize (la_open_bound k r); intros; ord.
Qed.

Lemma lvl_le_bound : forall k l l', lvl_le l l' -> la_fresh k (snd l') ->
    lvl_le (lvl_bound k l) (fst l', la_unwk k (snd l')).
Proof.
  intros k [c xs] [d ys] Hle Hys; cbn [fst snd] in *.
  pose proof (lvl_le_la_fresh _ _ _ Hle Hys) as Hs; cbn [fst snd] in Hs.
  pose proof (la_open_bound_le k xs d Hs) as Hb.
  rewrite lvl_le_correct in Hle |- *; intros ν Hν.

  revert Hle Hb Hf; generalize (la_ev ν' xs) (la_ev ν' ys) (la_ev ν' (la_fresh_part k xs)) (la_open_bound k xs); intros; ord.
Qed.

The Universe of a Π

A Π of a small domain and a small codomain is small, at the join of the domain's level and the bound of the codomain's (lvl_bound): the codomain's level lives under the domain, and an atom of it that mentions the bound variable is bounded by its sort. Any other Π is at the join of the universes of its parts (unf_max). This is the term the checker normalises: the join is not canonical, and normalising it is what makes the inferred universe a normal form.
Definition unf_pi_tm (u v : unf) : exp :=
