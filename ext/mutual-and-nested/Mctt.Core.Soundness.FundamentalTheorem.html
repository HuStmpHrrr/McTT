Mctt.Core.Soundness.FundamentalTheorem

The Fundamental Theorem of the Gluing Model

Every judgment of a global context holds in the gluing model, along every sound embedding (soundness_fundamental): a context glues, a term glues at its type, a universe is one, and every other judgment glues its context. By induction on the derivation, each rule is its gluing rule, stated and proved in the file of its construct; the case of each rule only applies its gluing rule.
The theorem then gives the rest. Each entry of a global context is valid where it is checked, so a well-formed global context embeds soundly into itself (glu_emb_refl), from what Global/Embedding.v makes of valid entries. At that embedding the gluing judgments are the gluing relations at the global context, as the corollaries below state them for contexts and terms. There is no part for substitutions, since the gluing model has no substitution judgment, and none for equalities, since the model relates a term to a value rather than two terms to each other.

Theorem soundness_fundamental :
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ, ⊢ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ -> ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ) /\
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ A M, Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A -> Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ M : A) /\
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ A M M', Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M ≈ M' : A -> ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ) /\
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ A A', Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A ⊆ A' -> ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ) /\
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ U, Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ᵤ U -> Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ᵤ U) /\
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ Ψ Ψ', Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ˣ Ψ ≈ Ψ' -> ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ) /\
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ U U', Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ᵘ U ≈ U' -> ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ) /\
  (forall Θ Ξ Γ H H', Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ᵐ H ≈ H' -> ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ).
Proof.

Section soundness_fundamental.
  Context {GC : GCtx}.

The identity is sound at a well-formed global context.
  Lemma glu_emb_refl_gc : ⊢ᵍ gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack -> glu_emb gc_deps gc_stack gc_deps gc_stack.
  Proof. exact (glu_emb_refl _ _). Qed.

  Corollary soundness_fundamental_ctx : forall Γ, ⊢ Γ -> ⊩ Γ.
  Proof.
    intros * H; destruct soundness_fundamental as (Kc & _).
    pose proof (Kc _ _ _ H _ _ (glu_emb_refl_gc (ctx_wf_gctx _ _ _ H))) as H'; destruct GC; exact H'.
  Qed.

  Corollary soundness_fundamental_exp : forall Γ M A, Γ ⊢ M : A -> Γ ⊩ M : A.
  Proof.
    intros * H; destruct soundness_fundamental as (_ & Ke & _).
    pose proof (Ke _ _ _ _ _ H _ _ (glu_emb_refl_gc (ctx_wf_gctx _ _ _ (presup_exp_ctx H)))) as H'; destruct GC; exact H'.
  Qed.
End soundness_fundamental.
