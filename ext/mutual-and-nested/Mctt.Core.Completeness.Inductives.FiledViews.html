The View Equations of a Filed Group, Read in the PER Model

The semantic view equations of a group (views_self_valid) are judgments over the context of its members as variables (grp_sctx). Once the group is filed at p, the substitution grp_sb p D of its type formers at the uniform parameters for those variables is built from valid parts (grp_sb_gsub), and moves each equation to the filed group: a view's instance is typed at the uniform parameters, and its non-uniform parameters and the arguments of its constructors are its container's at the instance. These are the semantic counterparts of the syntactic decl_typed_view_* lemmas of Core.Syntactic.System.Global.Presup.

The arguments of a view's constructor are its container's at the view's instance.
  Lemma filed_view_args : forall k Mk qc k' Is vm Dq i' Mi' v Mv c gs' gs, ind_member D k = Some Mk ->

      ind_lookup gc_deps gc_stack qc = Some Dq -> ind_member Dq i' = Some Mi' -> List.nth_error vm i' = Some v ->
      ind_member D v = Some Mv -> ind_con Mi' c = Some gs' -> ind_con Mv c = Some gs ->
      im_params D Mv ⊨ˣ tele_sub (cargs_ctx qc Dq Mi' gs') (sb_qn (im_nn Mv) (sb_of_list Id Is)) ≈ cargs_ctx p D Mv gs.

    pose proof (gsub_qn _ _ _ _ grp_sb_valid Hass' (rel_ctx_wf_app_r _ _ (rel_ext_under_ctx_left _ _ _ H))) as Hq'.

  Qed.
