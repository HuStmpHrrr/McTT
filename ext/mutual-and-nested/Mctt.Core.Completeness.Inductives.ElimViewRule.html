Mctt.Core.Completeness.Inductives.ElimViewRule

The Eliminator at a View, as Its Container's

The semantic rule E1′: the eliminator of a valid group at its view m of the container's member k' is the container's eliminator at k' with the wrapped blocks (vwrap). At a semantic substitution of values C1 and C2, the chain of four values links through the group's eliminator at C2: the group's eliminator against itself (elim_stage), and the container's at C2 and at the moved blocks against the group's at C2 (cross_elim_all, with its stuck eliminators read alike by cross_bot_all).

The value of an eliminator.
#[warnings="-cannot-define-projection"]
Record elim_exp_value (oq : option qname) (Us : list exp) (j : nat) (Bks : list eblock) (M : exp) (ρ : env) (r : domain) : Prop :=
  mk_elim_exp_value
  {
The uniform parameters' values
    eev_ups : list domain
    
The scrutinee's value
  ; eev_scrut : domain
    
The group eliminated
  ; eev_grp : qname
    
Us evaluate to eev_ups
  ; eev_ups_eval : ⟦ Us ⟧* gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ⍮ ρ ↘ eev_ups
    
M evaluates to eev_scrut
  ; eev_scrut_eval : ⟦ M ⟧ ρ ↘ eev_scrut
    
The group is the annotation's, or the scrutinee's
  ; eev_grp_of : elim_grp_of oq eev_scrut = Some eev_grp
    
The eliminator on the scrutinee's value gives r
  ; eev_elim : ⟦elim eev_scrut at j of eev_grp eev_ups Bks ⟧ gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ⍮ ρ ↘ r }.
