Mctt.Core.Completeness.LogicalRelation.Substitution

Building Semantic Substitutions

These lemmas validate no typing rule, since substitution is an operation and has no rules of its own. They are the tools the case files need: every rule with a premise in an extended context (Π, λ, ℕ-elim, …) is proved by instantiating that premise at a substitution built here.
The target context is always an extension, whose context PER is per_env_extend, so each proof has two halves:
  • Tails: the underlying substitution, from one instantiation of the hypothesis, or from two merged along a shared value.
  • Heads: the extension term, whose four values must land in the head PER of the target. That PER is the one A denotes at the substituted environments, while the hypothesis about the term speaks of A[σ] at the outer ones, so the two must be bridged. per_head is impredicative for this reason: bridging is then saturation of the per_univ_elem irrelevance biconditionals by handle_per_univ_elem_irrel.
The type of an extended context is written A and the extension term M, also because S is nat's constructor, so S[ψ]ʷ in a tactic argument would be read as a name and rejected.

The same for a substituted type. Every rule with a premise in an extended context needs this: q σ goes from Γ ▹ A[σ] to Δ ▹ A, and a Π-, λ- or ℕ-elim rule instantiates its premise there.

Lemma rel_exp_of_typ_extend_simple : forall {Γ A} {i : nat} {B B' j env_relΓ},

The Wk instance of the same, which is the only way a weakened term acquires a value. The equation ⟦M[φ]ʷ⟧ρ = ⟦M⟧(⟪φ⟫ ρ) fails: in the λ case the two sides are the closures λ ρ (N[q φ]ʷ) and λ (⟪φ⟫ ρ) N, which are different and not even related. So semantic weakening exists only as the instance of a judgment along Wk, and ⟦M[↑]ʷ⟧(ρ ↦ c) is reachable no other way. rel_exp_under_ctx_extend_simple is the Id instance of the same idea.
Unlike there, the element PER is not replaced by a canonical per_head. Instead B's four values are reported, with the PER at its outer pair (which per_head_iff turns into the head PER of B[↑]ʷ at the two extended environments) and at its inner pair (which per_pi_iff turns into the function PER of B at the two tails, when B is a Π). Which one a caller wants depends on the shape of B.

q Preserves Semantic Weakening

The only lemma here about weakenings, and a cheap one: both halves of the extended context PER hold by conversion for q ψ (eval_wk_q_tail, eval_wk_q_zero), so the tail obligation is the hypothesis and the head obligation is one bridging step.

Lemma rel_wk_under_ctx_q : forall {Γ ψ Δ A} {i : nat},
    Γ ⊨ʷ ψ : Δ ->
    Δ ⊨ A ≈ A : Typeω@i ->
    Γ ▹ A[ψ]ʷ ⊨ʷ (wk_q ψ) : Δ ▹ A.
Proof.
  intros * Hψj HA.
  pose proof Hψj as [env_relΓ HΓ env_relΔ HΔ Hψ].
  pose proof (rel_exp_of_typ_inversion HA) as [env_relΔ' [HΔ' HAgen]].
  handle_per_ctx_env_irrel.
The commutation obligation of A along ι ψ. It is used twice, to build the context PER of Γ ▹ A[ψ]ʷ and to bridge the head PERs, so it is named, at an environment pair the caller supplies.
  assert (Hcom : forall ρ ρ',
             Dom ρ ≈ ρ' ∈ env_relΓ ->
             rel_exp A (ι ψ) ρ ⟪ψ⟫ ρ
                     A (ι ψ) ρ' ⟪ψ⟫ ρ' (per_univ i))

    intros ρ ρ' Hρ.
    destruct (Hcom _ _ Hρ) as [a1 ? ? a4 Ha1 ? ? Ha4 Hchain].

Extension is Semantic

The first of three extension lemmas, and the template for the other two. At every Kripke stage Γ' ⊨ʷ φ : Γ it produces four environments and the chain relating them in the extended context PER of Δ ▹ A.
The four environments come from the two judgments about σ ,, M: the tails from the substitution judgment, the heads from the term judgment. sb_wk_extend says the two halves recombine into the substitution in the goal (eval_sub_wk_extend packages this).
The chain then splits link by link into tails and heads. The tails are already related by the substitution judgment's chain. The heads are related in the PER the term judgment produced, and the work is to identify that PER with the head PER of Δ, A. This needs A's values at all four environments linked, which takes two more instantiations of the type judgment: along sb_wk σ φ (rel_sub_under_ctx_wk) for the outer pair, and along σ for the inner pair. The type judgment's own chain links these to each other and to the term judgment's type chain, and handle_per_univ_elem_irrel then closes everything (solve_per_env_extend_chain).

Lemma rel_sub_under_ctx_extend : forall {Γ Δ σ σ' A} {i : nat} {M M'},
    Γ ⊨ˢ σ ≈ σ' : Δ ->
    Δ ⊨ A ≈ A : Typeω@i ->
    Γ ⊨ M ≈ M' : A[σ] ->
    Γ ⊨ˢ σ,,M ≈ σ',,M' : Δ ▹ A.
Proof.
  intros * Hσj HA HM.
  pose proof Hσj as [env_relΓ HΓ env_relΔ HΔ Hσ].
  pose proof (per_ctx_env_of_typ HΔ HA) as HΔA.
  pose proof (rel_exp_of_typ_inversion HA) as [env_relΔ' [HΔ' HAgen]].
  destruct HM as [env_relΓ2 HΓ2 j HMgen].
  handle_per_ctx_env_irrel.
  eexists_rel_sub.
  intros Γ' env_rel' HΓ' φ Hφ ρ ρ' Hρ.
  pose proof (rel_wk_under_ctx_intro HΓ' HΓ Hφ) as Hφj.
Tails, and the heads that go with them.
  destruct (Hσ _ _ HΓ' _ Hφ _ _ Hρ) as [t1 t2 t3 t4 Ht1 Ht2 Ht3 Ht4 Htails].
  destruct (HMgen _ _ HΓ' _ _ (rel_sub_of_wk Hφj) _ _ _ _ Hρ
                  (eval_sub_of_wk _ _) (eval_sub_of_wk _ _)) as [Rt [Htyp Hexp]].
  destruct Hexp as [m1 m2 m3 m4 Hm1 Hm2 Hm3 Hm4 Hheads].
  rewrite exp_sub_of_wk in Hm1, Hm4.
  apply (mk_rel_sub (t1 ↦ m1) (t2 ↦ m2) (t3 ↦ m3) (t4 ↦ m4));
    [ apply eval_sub_wk_extend; eassumption
    | apply eval_sub_extend; eassumption
    | apply eval_sub_extend; eassumption
    | apply eval_sub_wk_extend; eassumption
    | ].
The two instantiations of HA that link A's four values, and the term judgment's type chain, which links them to Rt. Rewriting the outer types with A[σ][ι φ] = A[sb_wk σ φ] makes the first instantiation's values the same values, not merely equal ones.
  pose proof (HAgen _ _ HΓ' _ _ (rel_sub_under_ctx_wk Hσj Hφj) _ _ _ _ Hρ Ht1 Ht4) as Houter.
  pose proof (HAgen _ _ HΓ _ _ Hσj _ _ _ _ (Hφ _ _ Hρ) Ht2 Ht3) as Hinner.
  destruct Htyp as [b1 b2 b3 b4 Hb1 Hb2 Hb3 Hb4 Hbchain].
  rewrite exp_sub_of_wk, exp_wk_sub in Hb1, Hb4.
  destruct Houter as [c1 c2 c3 c4 Hc1 Hc2 Hc3 Hc4 Hcchain].
  destruct Hinner as [d1 d2 d3 d4 Hd1 Hd2 Hd3 Hd4 Hdchain].
  functional_eval_rewrite_clear.
  destruct Hbchain as [? [? ?]].
  destruct_per_univ_chain Hcchain.
  destruct_per_univ_chain Hdchain.
  solve_per_env_extend_chain.
Qed.

Extension by a Precomposed Term

The same shape as rel_sub_under_ctx_extend, but the term judgment lives in Δ rather than Γ, so it is the one instantiated more than once; the type judgment is only needed to build the context PER of Δ ▹ T (via presup_rel_exp_under_ctx).
The two obvious instantiations, along sb_wk σ φ for the outer environments and along σ for the inner ones, are not enough: their chains live in different PERs and share no value (⟦M⟧t1 against ⟦M⟧t2). A third instantiation bridges them: the reflexive-left hypothesis Δ ⊨ M : T at Id ≈ Id and at the environment pair t1 ≈ t2 the tails provide. After exp_sub_id its chain is ⟦M⟧t1 twice and ⟦M⟧t2 twice, which links the first instantiation to the second. No extra instantiation of the type judgment is needed: each of the three term instantiations carries its own type chain, and those overlap the same way.
So three chains are combined into the four-value chain of the goal, by two applications of rel_chain_merge, which joins two chains along a shared value.

Lemma rel_sub_under_ctx_extend_sub : forall {Γ Δ σ σ' T M M'},

    Δ ⊨ M ≈ M' : T ->

  pose proof Hσj as [env_relΓ HΓ env_relΔ HΔ Hσ].
  pose proof (presup_rel_exp_under_ctx HM) as [i HT].

  pose proof (rel_exp_under_ctx_refl_left HM) as HMrefl.
  destruct HM as [env_relΔ2 HΔ2 j HMgen].
  destruct HMrefl as [env_relΔ3 HΔ3 j' HMrgen].
  handle_per_ctx_env_irrel.
  eexists_rel_sub.
  intros Γ' env_rel' HΓ' φ Hφ ρ ρ' Hρ.
  pose proof (rel_wk_under_ctx_intro HΓ' HΓ Hφ) as Hφj.

  destruct Hexp2 as [g1 g2 g3 g4 Hg1 Hg2 Hg3 Hg4 Hgchain].
  destruct Hexp3 as [h1 h2 h3 h4 Hh1 Hh2 Hh3 Hh4 Hhchain].

  rewrite <- exp_wk_sub in Hf1, Hf4.
  rewrite exp_sub_id in Hh1, Hh4.
  destruct Htyp1 as [a1 a2 a3 a4 Ha1 Ha2 Ha3 Ha4 Hachain].
  destruct Htyp2 as [b1 b2 b3 b4 Hb1 Hb2 Hb3 Hb4 Hbchain].

  rewrite exp_sub_id in Hc1, Hc4.
  functional_eval_rewrite_clear.
  apply (mk_rel_sub (t1 ↦ f1) (t2 ↦ g1) (t3 ↦ g4) (t4 ↦ f4));
    [ apply eval_sub_wk_extend; eassumption
    | apply eval_sub_extend; eassumption
    | apply eval_sub_extend; eassumption
    | apply eval_sub_wk_extend; eassumption
    | ].
The three type chains identify R1, R2 and R3 with each other.
  destruct Hachain as [? [? ?]].
  destruct Hbchain as [? [? ?]].
  destruct Hcchain as [? [? ?]].
  handle_per_univ_elem_irrel.
Then the three term chains become one. Neither merge can be inferred: the shared value is ⟦M⟧t1 for the first and ⟦M⟧t2 for the second, and the intermediate list must mention both.

  assert (Hm2 : rel_chain R1 ([f1; g1; g4; f4])) by (merge_rel_chain Hm1 Hgchain g2).
  clear Hfchain Hgchain Hhchain Hm1.
  solve_per_env_extend_chain.

The Type Judgment of an Instantiation

The counterpart of rel_typ_of_pi for the other shape a type takes in an elimination rule: B[Id ,, N], the codomain of a Π at the applied argument, or the motive of a ℕ-eliminator at the scrutinee. Every such rule needs the same four values and element PER, so they are computed once here.
The value of B[Id ,, N] at ρ is not ⟦B⟧(ρ ↦ ⟦N⟧ρ): the two differ as soon as B builds a closure, which captures the environment it is evaluated in, and B = λ C #1 already separates them. So the four values are reached through two instantiations of the codomain judgment:
  • along σ ,, N[σ] ≈ σ' ,, N[σ'], whose outer values are the goal's outer ones, since B[Id ,, N][σ] = B[σ ,, N[σ]] (exp_sub_extend_sub);
  • along Id ,, N ≈ Id ,, N at the substituted pair of environments, whose outer values are the goal's inner ones.
The two share no value, so a third instance bridges them: the codomain at the pair of arguments crossing from one to the other. Two merges give the chain from which the goal's four values are selected; this is the three-chain shape of rel_sub_under_ctx_extend_sub, for the same reason.
The element PER is the head PER of B at the arguments the type names, ⟦N⟧(⟦σ⟧ρ) and ⟦N⟧(⟦σ'⟧ρ'). A consumer's chains arrive in the head PER of the arguments they were read at, and per_head_of_args moves them. For that it needs the codomain at an arbitrary related pair, which is therefore reported too, as are N's four values and their domain PER, from which every consumer draws its argument pairs.
