What the Two Checkers Share

The extracted checker (Extraction.TypeCheck) and the reference checker it refines (Reference.TypeCheck) recurse along the same orders, decide the same side conditions, carry the same initial environment, and prove their obligations with the same tactics, which are therefore defined here, outside any section, rather than locally to one checker.

  Definition tele_ass_dec : forall Δ, { tele_ass Δ } + { ~ tele_ass Δ }.
  Proof.
    induction Δ as [| [A | A M | U | A M U] Δ IH].
    - left; constructor.
    - destruct IH as [H | H]; [ left; constructor; eauto | right; intros Hf; inversion Hf; contradiction ].
    - right; intros Hf; inversion Hf as [| ? ? He _]; destruct He; discriminate.
    - right; intros Hf; inversion Hf as [| ? ? He _]; destruct He; discriminate.
    - right; intros Hf; inversion Hf as [| ? ? He _]; destruct He; discriminate.

  Definition entry_shape_dec : forall E E', { entry_shape E E' } + { ~ entry_shape E E' }.
  Proof.
    intros [b pv A [M |] | pm U | pvi Di Φi | pd ? ? ? | ? ? ?] [b' pv' A' [M' |] | pm' U' | pvi' Di' Φi' | pd' ? ? ? | ? ? ?]; cbn;
      try (right; intros []; fail); try (left; exact I).
    - destruct b, b', (Bool.bool_dec pv pv'); try (right; intuition congruence; fail); left; auto.
    - exact (Bool.bool_dec pm pm').
    - exact (Bool.bool_dec pd pd').
  Defined.

The Initial Environment the Checker Carries

Every normalization the checker does is at the initial environment of its context, or rather at an environment related to it entry by entry (fast_env), which the fast evaluation computes. The checker computes it once, where it enters a context, and extends it entry by entry under binders, rather than once per normalization (nbe_ty_env_impl).
  Definition tenv G := fast_env gc_deps gc_stack G.

  Lemma tenv_order_of_wf : forall G, ⊢ G -> initial_env_order gc_deps gc_stack G.
  Proof.
    intros G HG.
    assert (G ⊢ ⊤ : Typeω@0) as [W [HW _]]%soundness_ty by mauto 2.
    inversion HW; subst; eauto using initial_env_order_sound.
  Qed.
