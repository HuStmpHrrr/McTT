Prelude›Algebra›Magma

Magmas: IsMagma A eq op holds when eq is an equivalence on A and op respects it in both arguments; the bundle Magma is a carrier, a relation, an operation and that predicate.

A magma is the bottom of the algebraic hierarchy of Prelude›Algebra, which has two layers:

&bull; a predicate record IsX A eq ops, over a carrier, a relation and the operations, holds the laws of the structure X, stated up to eq, and extends the predicate below by a field: IsMonoid A eq op e has isSemigroup : IsSemigroup A eq op;

&bull; a bundle X holds a carrier, the relation, the operations and the predicate: Monoid is carrier, eq, op, e and isMonoid. A structure with two operations states the laws of each through the predicates at the one carrier: IsLattice A eq meet join has meetIsSemilattice : IsSemilattice A eq meet and joinIsSemilattice : IsSemilattice A eq join, so the carrier is shared by construction. The inherited laws are forwarded, in the with-areas, by open … export. The levels c and r are those of the carrier and of the relation, Prelude›Algebra›Setoid.

Under Leibniz equality every operation is a congruence, so Prelude›Algebra›Leibniz.idIsMagma A op fills the bottom predicate of a Leibniz instance in one line.

module Prelude›Algebra›Magma (c : Level) (r : Level) where

import Prelude›Algebra›Setoid c r use (IsEquivalence; Setoid)

record IsMagma (A : Type@{c}) (eq : ∀ (x : A) (y : A) → Type@{r})
    (op : ∀ (x : A) (y : A) → A) : Type@{maxl c r} where
  isEquivalence : IsEquivalence A eq;
  opCong : ∀ (a : A) (b : A) (c : A) (d : A) (hac : eq a c) (hbd : eq b d) →
    eq (op a b) (op c d)
with
  open isEquivalence export (refl; sym; trans)
end

record Magma : Type@{succl (maxl c r)} where
  carrier : Type@{c};
  eq : ∀ (x : carrier) (y : carrier) → Type@{r};
  op : ∀ (x : carrier) (y : carrier) → carrier;
  isMagma : IsMagma carrier eq op
with
  open isMagma export (refl; sym; trans; opCong)
  def setoid : Setoid ≔
    record Setoid where
      carrier ≔ carrier; eq ≔ eq; isEquivalence ≔ isMagma.isEquivalence
    end
  end
end

end
