Prelude›Algebra›Magma

Magmas: a carrier carrier in Type@{u} with a binary operation op. A magma is the bottom layer of the bundled algebraic hierarchy of Prelude›Algebra: each structure above it is a record with one field holding the structure below and further fields for its operations and laws. The laws are Leibniz equalities, Prelude›Logic›Leibniz.

The record holds a type of Type@{u}, so it lives in Type@{succl u}.

module Prelude›Algebra›Magma (u : Level) where

record Magma : Type@{succl u} where
  carrier : Type@{u};
  op : ∀ (x : carrier) (y : carrier) → carrier
end

end
