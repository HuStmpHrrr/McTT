Prelude›Data›Parity

Parities: even and odd, the integers modulo 2, with the opposite parity inv, addition add and multiplication mul, the parity of a number, and the isomorphism toSign with the signs of Prelude›Data›Sign. Prelude›Arith›Parity has the predicates Even and Odd on numbers instead. The laws are in Prelude›Data›Parity›Properties.

module Prelude›Data›Parity where

import Prelude›Builtin›Nat
import Prelude›Data›Sign use (Sign)
open Sign use (negative; positive)

inductive Parity : Type@0 where
| even
| odd
end

open Parity use (even; odd)

def inv (p : Parity) : Parity ≔
  elim p at z -> Parity | even ⇒ odd | odd ⇒ even end
end

def add (p : Parity) (q : Parity) : Parity ≔
  elim p at z -> Parity | even ⇒ q | odd ⇒ inv q end
end

def mul (p : Parity) (q : Parity) : Parity ≔
  elim p at z -> Parity | even ⇒ even | odd ⇒ q end
end

The parity of a number: each successor flips it.

def parity (n : ℕ) : Parity ≔
  elim n at z -> Parity | zero ⇒ even | succ k, r ⇒ inv r end
end

even is positive and odd is negative: addition of parities is multiplication of signs.

def toSign (p : Parity) : Sign ≔
  elim p at z -> Sign | even ⇒ positive | odd ⇒ negative end
end

def fromSign (s : Sign) : Parity ≔
  elim s at z -> Parity | negative ⇒ odd | positive ⇒ even end
end

end
