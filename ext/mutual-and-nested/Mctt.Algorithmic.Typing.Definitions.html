nfs_agree Γ Ms Rs Ts: the terms Ms and Rs have, position by position, the same normal form at the types Ts. The eliminator's rule compares by it the uniform parameters its member's type has at the eliminator's uniform parameters with those of the type its scrutinee infers.
Inductive nfs_agree (Γ : ctx) : list exp -> list exp -> list typ -> Prop :=
No term
| nfs_agree_nil : nfs_agree Γ nil nil nil
The first terms have the normal form W at the first type, then the rest agree
| nfs_agree_cons : forall M R T W Ms Rs Ts,
    nbe_f Γ M T W ->
    nbe_f Γ R T W ->
    nfs_agree Γ Ms Rs Ts ->
    nfs_agree Γ (M :: Ms) (R :: Rs) (T :: Ts).

alg_ty_conv Γ A B: the types A and B have one normal form.
Definition alg_ty_conv (Γ : ctx) (A B : typ) : Prop := exists W, nbe_ty_f Γ A W /\ nbe_ty_f Γ B W.

A Π is at the join of the universes of its parts (unf_pi_tm): a large universe absorbs a small one, and two small ones join on levels, the codomain's bounded under the binder (lvl_bound): an atom that mentions the bound variable is bounded by its sort. The join is the normal form of that universe, a side condition rather than the conclusion's index: an index computed from the premises makes the judgment's inversion non-terminating. The side conditions on the parts are is_univ_nf rather than equations on a deciding function, for the reason given there. The codomain's universe is taken normalised (UB'): the bound trusts the sorts its atoms carry, and those of a normal form are the sorts of the atoms' types. (An inferred type is already normal, alg_type_infer_normal, so UB' is UB; the premise is what lets soundness, which that theorem rests on, see it.)

The two instances of ati_let, with an annotation and without one.
Section LetInstances.
  Context {GC : GCtx}.

  Lemma ati_let_some : forall Γ A UA u M B C D,
      Γ ⊢a A ⟹ UA ->
      is_univ_nf UA u ->
      Γ ⊢a M ⟸ A ->
      Γ ▸ A ≔ M ⊢a B ⟹ C ->
      nbe_ty_f Γ C[Id,,M] D ->
      Γ ⊢a ℓ A ≔ M in B ⟹ D.
  Proof.
    intros * HA Hu HM HB HD.
    eapply ati_let with (W := UA);
      [ right; reflexivity | intros _; exact HA | intros _; exact Hu | intros _; exact HM
      | discriminate | discriminate | exact HB | exact HD ].
  Qed.

  Lemma ati_let_none : forall Γ M (A : nf) B C D,
      Γ ⊢a M ⟹ A ->
      Γ ▸ (A : exp) ≔ M ⊢a B ⟹ C ->
      nbe_ty_f Γ C[Id,,M] D ->
      Γ ⊢a ℓ ≔ M in B ⟹ D.
  Proof.
    intros * HM HB HD.
    eapply ati_let with (W := A) (UA := A) (u := unl 0);
      [ left; reflexivity | discriminate | discriminate | discriminate
      | intros _; exact HM | intros _; reflexivity | exact HB | exact HD ].
  Qed.
End LetInstances.
