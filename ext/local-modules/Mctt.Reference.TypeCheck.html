The Reference Checker

The direct implementation of the algorithmic judgments, rule by rule: an application normalizes the instantiated codomain B[Id,,N] of the head's type, and a check normalizes both types it compares. It is not extracted; Extraction.TypeCheck is the checker that is, and Reference.Refinement proves that the two compute the same. The parts they share (orders, decisions of side conditions, the initial environment, the obligation tactics) are in Extraction.TypeCheckBase.
