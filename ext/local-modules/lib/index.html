The McTT library

Prelude

  • Prelude›Algebra›Group

    Groups over a setoid.

  • Prelude›Algebra›Instances

    Instances of Prelude›Algebra›Monoid: numbers under addition, multiplication and maximum, and functions on numbers under composition.

  • Prelude›Algebra›Integers

    The integers, as differences of numbers.

  • Prelude›Algebra›Monoid

    Monoids over a setoid.

  • Prelude›Arith›Ackermann

    The Ackermann function, by higher-order primitive recursion, with its defining equations and closed forms for ack 1 and ack 2.

  • Prelude›Arith›Binary

    Binary representation of numbers.

  • Prelude›Arith›Binomial›Properties

    Laws of Prelude›Arith›Binomial: the edges of Pascal's triangle, vanishing above the diagonal, Pascal's rule, symmetry, and the row sum sumTo (choose n) (succ n) = pow 2 n.

  • Prelude›Arith›Binomial

    Binomial coefficients choose n k, computed by Pascal's recursion.

  • Prelude›Arith›Decide

    Decidability of equality and order on natural numbers, a boolean-style equality test eqb with its specification and case analysis, and the conditional pick on such tests.

  • Prelude›Arith›Div

    Euclidean division.

  • Prelude›Arith›Divides

    Divisibility, decided by the remainder of Prelude›Arith›Div, with its basic laws and its link to Prelude›Arith›Parity.

  • Prelude›Arith›Equality

    Equality of natural numbers, computed by recursion on both sides, with its basic laws.

  • Prelude›Arith›Factorial

    The factorial, with its defining equations, positivity, monotonicity in one step, and the bound pow 2 n <= factorial (succ n).

  • Prelude›Arith›Fibonacci›Properties

    Laws of Prelude›Arith›Fibonacci: the defining recurrence, positivity, monotonicity, the sum identity and the addition formula.

  • Prelude›Arith›Fibonacci

    The Fibonacci numbers, computed in linear time by iterating on a pair of consecutive values.

  • Prelude›Arith›Gcd›Properties

    Laws of Prelude›Arith›Gcd: the values at 0, 1 and the diagonal, independence from the fuel, divisibility of both arguments, the greatest divisor property, and commutativity.

  • Prelude›Arith›Gcd

    The greatest common divisor, by Euclid's algorithm.

  • Prelude›Arith›Induction

    Derived induction principles on natural numbers: case analysis, two-step induction and strong (course-of-values) induction, all from rec, for motives of any level.

  • Prelude›Arith›Lattice

    The natural numbers as a distributive lattice under Prelude›Arith›MinMax.

  • Prelude›Arith›MinMax

    Minimum and maximum, by recursion on both arguments, with their laws stated with Prelude›Arith›Equality.

  • Prelude›Arith›Monotone

    Monotonicity of addition, multiplication and subtraction with respect to Prelude›Arith›Order.

  • Prelude›Arith›Mult

    Multiplication and its laws, stated with Prelude›Arith›Equality and proved from the laws of Prelude›Arith›Plus.

  • Prelude›Arith›Order

    The order on natural numbers, computed by recursion on both sides like Prelude›Arith›Equality.

  • Prelude›Arith›Parity

    Parity of natural numbers: Even and Odd computed by recursion, with doubling and the basic laws.

  • Prelude›Arith›Plus

    Addition and its laws, stated with Prelude›Arith›Equality.

  • Prelude›Arith›Pow

    Exponentiation by recursion on the exponent, with its laws stated with Prelude›Arith›Equality and proved from those of Prelude›Arith›Mult.

  • Prelude›Arith›Prime

    Prime numbers by trial division.

  • Prelude›Arith›Sub

    Predecessor and truncated subtraction, with their laws stated with Prelude›Arith›Equality.

  • Prelude›Arith›Sum

    Finite sums sumTo f n of f i for i < n, with linearity laws, sums of constants, and the closed forms of Gauss and of the odd numbers.

  • Prelude›Data›Church›Properties

    Laws of Prelude›Data›Church, at every level u: the arithmetic of numerals agrees with that of ℕ, moving between levels keeps the number, and the length and sum of lists.

  • Prelude›Data›Church

    Church encodings at a universe level: numerals, booleans and lists.

  • Prelude›Data›Stream›Properties

    Laws of Prelude›Data›Stream on streams of numbers, stated pointwise with Prelude›Arith›Equality, and the stream nats: elements of the operations, the scan by addition as prefix sums of Prelude›Arith›Sum, interleaving at even and odd indices of Prelude›Arith›Parity, and sums of taken prefixes.

  • Prelude›Data›Stream

    Infinite streams over A, encoded as functions from indices: Stream is ∀ (n : ℕ) → A.

  • Prelude›Data›Vec›Properties

    Laws of Prelude›Data›Vec, stated with Prelude›Arith›Equality on number-valued observations: elements read with nth, sums of vectors of numbers, and the answers of the tests allb and anyb.

  • Prelude›Data›Vec

    Vectors of length n over A, encoded as functions from bounded indices: Vec n is ∀ (i : ℕ) (h : Lt i n) → A.

  • Prelude›Function›Combinators

    Generic combinators over a type parameter at any universe level, with their laws at ℕ.

  • Prelude›Function›Iter

    Iteration of an arbitrary function: a unit parameterized by the level u of the type, the type A and the function, so each client instantiates it as it needs: Iter.

  • Prelude›Logic

    Propositions as types, at every universe level: ⊤ holds, ⊥ has no proof; negation, Church-encoded conjunction, disjunction, equivalence and existence over numbers, and decidability.

  • Prelude›Universe›Formers

    Type formers at universe levels.

  • Prelude›Universe›Levels

    Universe levels as data.

Programs

  • Algebra

    A client of Prelude›Algebra›Monoid and its instances.

  • Arithmetic

    A client of the arithmetic in Prelude: closed instances of the laws of addition and multiplication, and a few computations.

  • Binary

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Binary: halves, low bits and binary digits of numbers, the number they write, the count of ones, and exponentiation by squaring.

  • Combinatorics

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Binomial and Prelude›Arith›Fibonacci: the first rows of Pascal's triangle and of the Fibonacci numbers, and closed instances of their laws, which normalize to ⋆.

  • Division

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Div and Prelude›Arith›Divides: computed quotients and remainders, and closed instances of their theorems.

  • Groups

    A client of Prelude›Algebra›Group, read in the group of integers of Prelude›Algebra›Integers.

  • Induction

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Induction and Prelude›Arith›Decide: halving by strong and by two-step induction, decisions eliminated into ℕ to show which branch they compute, and the equality test eqb.

  • Lattice

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Monotone and Prelude›Arith›Lattice: closed instances of the order and lattice laws normalize to ⋆, and a few computations with min, max and sub.

  • NatTheory

    A client of the Prelude library: Prelude›Arith›Equality, Prelude›Arith›Plus and Prelude›Function›Iter are separate units, loaded once each.

  • NumberTheory

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Gcd, its laws, and Prelude›Arith›Prime: greatest common divisors, the primes below 30, and closed instances of the theorems, which normalize to ⋆.

  • OrderParity

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Order and Prelude›Arith›Parity: closed instances of their theorems normalize to ⋆, and leb decides the order.

  • Polynomials

    Polynomials with natural coefficients, evaluated at a point.

  • Powers

    A client of Prelude›Arith›Pow, Prelude›Arith›Sum and Prelude›Arith›Factorial: a few computations, and closed instances of their theorems, which normalize to ⋆.

  • Programs

    A client of the programs in the Prelude library: subtraction, minimum and maximum, generic combinators, and the Ackermann function, run on closed instances.

  • Streams

    A client of Prelude›Data›Stream and its laws: the natural numbers, the even and odd numbers, the squares and two Fibonacci-like streams are read with nth and summed through take, and closed instances of the laws normalize to ⋆.

  • Tutorial

    A tour of the McTT surface language, one feature at a time.

  • Universes

    A client of the level-polymorphic Prelude: Prelude›Universe›Levels, Prelude›Universe›Formers, Prelude›Data›Church and its laws, and the level parameters of Prelude›Logic, Prelude›Data›Vec, Prelude›Function›Iter and Prelude›Arith›Induction.

  • Vectors

    A client of Prelude›Data›Vec and Prelude›Data›Vec›Properties: small vectors of numbers are built, mapped, zipped, appended, split, reversed, folded, read and summed, and closed instances of the laws normalize to ⋆.
