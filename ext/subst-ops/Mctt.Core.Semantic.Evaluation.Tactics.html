Substitution and weakening are now *operations*, so M[σ] is not in constructor form even when M is: an evaluation hypothesis ⟦ Type@i[σ] ⟧ ρ ↘ a cannot be inverted until exp_sub has been unfolded far enough to expose the head. Restricting the cbn delta list to the two recursive functions does exactly that and nothing else — in particular eval_sub (simpl never) and sb_q (simpl never) stay folded, so the encoding of lifting is never exposed.
