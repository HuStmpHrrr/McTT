Mctt.Core.Completeness.Global.Embedding

Valid Global Contexts

What an entry of a global context is worth at a target (valid_entry, valid_mod_tele, valid_ind_decl_entry), and the embedding that the validity of every entry gives: what the source files and has open is valid at the target, read from the source (valid_filed_mods_of_valid), and so are its declarations (valid_gctx_embedding_of, valid_gctx_embedding_of_bodies_valid). A unit valid read at a global context stays valid read at a bigger one (valid_unit_grow_all). Nothing here uses the fundamental theorem; that each entry is valid where it is checked, and so that a well-formed global context embeds soundly into itself, is proved with it in FundamentalTheorem.v.

4. What an entry is worth at the target

What an entry other than a body module is worth at Θ2 ⍮ Ξ2, read from Θs ⍮ Ξs: a definition, checked over T, is the Π and λ over T of a valid type and of a valid body of it; an alias is a valid unit in the empty context, read from any well-formed Θs ⍮ Ξs. A definition with a module part is valid as its two views, a transparent definition and an alias (ge_valid), so it asks nothing more here. The modules of inductive and record types are bodies; their declarations are valid as valid_ind_decl_entry and valid_record_decl_entry say.
A definition of kind r, of type A and body oM, stored over the telescope T, at Θ2 ⍮ Ξ2: A and oM are the Π at r and the λ over T of a type over T of the sort of r (abs_kind_sort: a universe, Prop, or the types of levels, as wf_gentry_def asks) and of a body of it; the Πs over T into r are formed (pi_kinds).
#[warnings="-cannot-define-projection"]
Record valid_def_entry (Θ2 : gdeps) (Ξ2 : gstack) (T : ctx) (r : abs_kind) (A : exp) (oM : option exp) : Prop := mk_valid_def_entry
  {
The type over the telescope
    valid_def_entry_type : exp
    
A universe index: the type is of Typeω@valid_def_entry_level at ak_relevant; at the other kinds the sort does not mention it
  ; valid_def_entry_level : nat
    
The body over the telescope, if any
  ; valid_def_entry_body : option exp
    
The stored type is the Π at r over the telescope of the type
  ; valid_def_entry_type_eq : A = ctx_pi_at r T valid_def_entry_type
    
The stored body is the λ over the telescope of the body
  ; valid_def_entry_body_eq : oM = option_map (ctx_fn T) valid_def_entry_body
    
The type is a valid type of the sort of r
  ; valid_def_entry_typed : @rel_exp_under_ctx (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) T (abs_kind_sort r Typeω@valid_def_entry_level)
                              valid_def_entry_type valid_def_entry_type
    
The body is a valid term of the type
  ; valid_def_entry_body_typed : forall M0, valid_def_entry_body = Some M0 ->
      @rel_exp_under_ctx (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) T valid_def_entry_type M0 M0
    
The Πs over the telescope into r are formed: at ak_level every parameter is a level (level_sorted_parameters)
  ; valid_def_entry_pi_kinds : forall Δ1 κ B Δ2, T = Δ1 ++ ce_ass κ B :: Δ2 -> pi_kinds κ r }.

Definition valid_entry (Θs : gdeps) (Ξs : gstack) (Θ2 : gdeps) (Ξ2 : gstack) (T : ctx) (E : gentry) : Prop :=
  match E with
  | ge_def _ _ r A oM => valid_def_entry Θ2 Ξ2 T r A oM
  | ge_mod _ U => ⊢ᵍ Θs ⍮ Ξs -> @valid_unit (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) Θs Ξs ⋅ (me_lit U)
  | ge_ind _ _ _ _ _ => True
  | ge_def_mod _ _ _ _ => True
  | ge_record _ _ _ _ => True
  end.

The telescope of a module: valid, of parameters only, and those relevant in the PER model, as modules take relevant parameters.
Record valid_mod_tele (Θ2 : gdeps) (Ξ2 : gstack) (T : ctx) : Prop := mk_valid_mod_tele
  {
The telescope is valid
    valid_mod_tele_wf : @rel_ctx_wf (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) T
    
It has parameters only
  ; valid_mod_tele_all_assumptions : all_assumptions T
    
They are types of a universe at one level
  ; valid_mod_tele_evaluated : exists l : nat, @rel_params_evaluated (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) nil T l }.

A declaration: valid, and the type of each member's type former, abstracted over the outer part of its parameters, a type over the inner part.
Definition valid_ind_decl_entry (Θ2 : gdeps) (Ξ2 : gstack) (D : ind_decl) : Prop :=
  @valid_ind_decl (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) D /\
  forall k Mk Δ1 Δ2, ind_member D k = Some Mk -> im_params D Mk = Δ1 ++ Δ2 -> exists i,
    @rel_exp_under_ctx (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) Δ2 (Typeω@i) (ctx_pi Δ1 (ind_sort D)[wk_shiftn (im_nn Mk)]ʷ)
      (ctx_pi Δ1 (ind_sort D)[wk_shiftn (im_nn Mk)]ʷ).

A record declaration: valid.
Definition valid_record_decl_entry (Θ2 : gdeps) (Ξ2 : gstack) (R : record_decl) : Prop :=
  @valid_record_decl (gc_mk Θ2 Ξ2) R.

The λ over a telescope at an environment, applied to arguments related at the telescope, against its body at the telescope's own extension of a related environment by them. An application stores each argument at its type's value (eval_app_fn), the telescope's extension at its type's closure (env_tele): the two are related through the body's judgment. Lemma ctx_fn_apps_tele : forall T Γ RΓ B M r l k, all_assumptions T -> ⊨ T ++ Γ -> EF Γ ≈ Γ ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ RΓ ->

    forall ρ1 ρ2 ns g m, RΓ ρ1 ρ2 -> per_tele_args (rev T) ρ1 ρ1 ns ns -> List.length ns = List.length T ->
      ⟦ ctx_fn T M ⟧ ρ2 ↘ g -> ⟦ M ⟧ env_tele ρ1 (rev T) ns ↘ m ->
      exists v, $*| g & ns | gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ↘ v /\ per_head B B (env_tele ρ1 (rev T) ns) (env_tele ρ1 (rev T) ns) m v.
Proof.

  - destruct ns; cbn in Hl; [| discriminate ]; cbn [List.app ctx_fn rev] in *.
    change (env_tele ρ1 nil nil) with ρ1 in Hm |- *.
    destruct (valid_at _ _ _ _ _ HM HB (ex_intro _ r (conj Hev HBk)) HΓ _ _ Hρ) as (m1 & v1 & a & i & R & Hm1 & Hv1 & Ha & HRa & Hmv).
    functional_eval_rewrite_clear.
    exists g; split; [ constructor |].
