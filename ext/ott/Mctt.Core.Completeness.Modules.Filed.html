Filed Modules in the PER Model

A global body module at arguments related at its telescope is related to itself in the module PER (gval_rel). A filed module at arguments valid against its telescope is valid (filed_valid), lacks the next parameter of the telescope (filed_lacks), and its term members are valid at the types they are looked up at (filed_mem).
