Semantic Contexts of Member Types

The canonical type of a member is built from the unit's parameters, the body before the member and the member's own type. This file holds what the member lemmas need of the contexts these are checked in: extension by slots and by definitions with a module part, the inversions of a valid context's entries, the validity of the two ends of a gsub, and the generalization of a type over a telescope (valid_exp_under_ctx_ctx_pi).

Semantic Contexts


Lemma rel_ctx_wf_extend_mod : forall {Γ U}, ⊨ Γ -> Γ ⊨ᵘ U ≈ U -> gunit_scoped (List.length Γ) U -> ⊨ Γ ▹ₘ U.
Proof.
  intros * HΓ [HU HsU _] HUs.
  destruct (rel_ctx_wf_per_ctx_env HΓ) as [R HR].
  econstructor; [ exact HΓ | exact (per_ctx_env_of_mod HR HU HUs HUs) | exact HU | exact HsU ].
Qed.

Lemma rel_ctx_wf_extend_dm : forall {Γ A M U} {i : nat},
    ⊨ Γ -> Γ ⊨ A : Typeω@i -> Γ ⊨ M : A -> Γ ⊨ᵘ U ≈ U -> gunit_scoped (List.length Γ) U -> ⊨ Γ ▸ₘ A ≔ M ‖ U.
Proof.
  intros * HΓ HA HM [HU HsU _] HUs.
  destruct (rel_ctx_wf_per_ctx_env HΓ) as [R HR].
  econstructor; [ exact HΓ | exact (per_ctx_env_of_dm HR HA HM HU HUs) | exact HA | exact HM | exact HU | exact HsU ].
Qed.

Lemma sem_ctx_extend_mod :
  `( Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ᵘ U ≈ U ->
     ⊨ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▹ₘ U ).
Proof.
  intros * HU; sem_intro.
  apply valid_ctx_mod;
    [ assumption | eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_mod; [ eassumption | eassumption | eapply valid_unit_lit_scoped; eassumption ]
    | assumption ].
Qed.

Lemma sem_ctx_extend_dm :
  `( Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ A : Typeω@i ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ M : A ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ᵘ U ≈ U ->
     ⊨ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▸ₘ A ≔ M ‖ U ).
Proof.
  intros * HA HM HU; sem_intro.
  apply valid_ctx_dm; [ assumption | eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_dm; [ eassumption | eassumption | eassumption | eassumption | eapply valid_unit_lit_scoped; eassumption ] | assumption ].
Qed.

The type of an assumption is a type of a sort of the assumption's kind.
Lemma rel_ctx_wf_ass_inv : forall {Γ κ A}, ⊨ Γ ▹{κ} A -> exists Srt, sort_kind Srt κ /\ Γ ⊨ A : Srt.
Proof. intros * H; inversion H; subst; eauto. Qed.

So it is of the assumption's kind.
Corollary rel_ctx_wf_ass_kind : forall {Γ κ A}, ⊨ Γ ▹{κ} A -> rel_type_kind Γ A κ.
Proof.
  intros * H; destruct (rel_ctx_wf_ass_inv H) as (Srt & HS & HA).
  exact (rel_type_kind_of_sort_kind HS HA).
Qed.

And so is that of a definition, its body valid at it.
Lemma rel_ctx_wf_def_inv : forall {Γ A M}, ⊨ Γ ▸ A ≔ M ->
    (exists Srt, type_sort Srt /\ Γ ⊨ A : Srt) /\ Γ ⊨ M : A.
Proof. intros * H; inversion H; subst; eauto. Qed.

The type of an assumption is a type of every index from some index on.
Corollary rel_ctx_wf_ass_type : forall {Γ κ A}, ⊨ Γ ▹{κ} A ->
    exists i, forall j, i <= j -> Γ ⊨ᵗ A ≈ A at j.
Proof.
  intros * H; destruct (rel_ctx_wf_ass_inv H) as (Srt & HS & HA).
  exact (rel_type_of_sort_ge (sort_kind_type_sort _ _ HS) HA).
Qed.

In particular it is a type of an index.
Corollary rel_ctx_wf_ass_typ : forall {Γ κ A}, ⊨ Γ ▹{κ} A -> exists i, Γ ⊨ᵗ A ≈ A at i.
Proof. intros * H; destruct (rel_ctx_wf_ass_type H) as [i Hi]; exists i; exact (Hi i (le_n i)). Qed.

Lemma rel_ctx_wf_mod_inv : forall {Γ U}, ⊨ Γ ▹ₘ U -> Γ ⊨ᵘ U ≈ U.
Proof. intros * H; inversion H; subst; constructor; assumption. Qed.

Lemma rel_ctx_wf_dm_inv : forall {Γ A M U}, ⊨ Γ ▸ₘ A ≔ M ‖ U ->
    (exists i, Γ ⊨ A : Typeω@i) /\ Γ ⊨ M : A /\ Γ ⊨ᵘ U ≈ U.
Proof. intros * H; inversion H; subst; repeat split; eauto; constructor; assumption. Qed.

A context entry's unit is valid where the entry is.
Lemma rel_ctx_wf_unit_inv : forall {Γ e U}, ⊨ e :: Γ -> centry_unit e = Some U -> Γ ⊨ᵘ U ≈ U.
Proof.
  intros * H He; destruct e; cbn in He; try discriminate; injection He as <-.
  - exact (rel_ctx_wf_mod_inv H).
  - exact (proj2 (proj2 (rel_ctx_wf_dm_inv H))).
Qed.

The context entry of a body entry with a module part holds its unit.
Lemma body_ctx_unit : forall Φ x E U, entry_unit E = Some U ->
    exists e, body_ctx (gm_ext Φ x E) = e :: body_ctx Φ /\ centry_unit e = Some U.
Proof.
  intros * HE; destruct E as [| pv U' | | pv A M U' |]; cbn in HE; try discriminate;
    injection HE as <-; eexists; split; reflexivity.
Qed.

Lemma gsub_sem : forall {Γ τ Δ}, gsub Γ τ Δ -> ⊨ Γ /\ ⊨ Δ.
Proof.
  induction 1 as [? HΓ | ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? [IH1 IH2] HS HB HN _ | ? ? ? ? ? ? ? [IH1 IH2] HS HB HM _
                 | ? ? ? ? ? [IH1 IH2] HU HUs | ? ? ? ? ? ? ? ? [IH1 IH2] HB HM HU _ HUs
                 | ? ? ? ? ? [IH1 IH2] Eq | ? ? ? ? ? ? HG [IH1 IH2] HS HB
                 | ? ? ? ? ? ? HG [IH1 IH2] HS HB HM | ? ? ? ? HG [IH1 IH2] HU HU' HUs
                 | ? ? ? ? ? ? ? HG [IH1 IH2] HB HM HU HU' HUs
                 | ? ? ? ? ? ? [IH1 IH2] HΓ Hσ Hl ];
    try (split; assumption).
  - split; [ assumption | eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_sort; eassumption ].
  - split; [ assumption | eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_def_sort; eassumption ].
  - split; [ assumption | eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_mod; eassumption ].
  - split; [ assumption | eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_dm; eassumption ].
  - pose proof (rel_exp_under_ctx_gsub HG HB) as H; rewrite (type_sort_sub _ _ (sort_kind_type_sort _ _ HS)) in H.
    split; eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_sort; [ exact IH1 | exact HS | exact H | exact IH2 | exact HS | exact HB ].
  - pose proof (rel_exp_under_ctx_gsub HG HB) as H1; rewrite (type_sort_sub _ _ HS) in H1.
    pose proof (rel_exp_under_ctx_gsub HG HM) as H2.
    split; eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_def_sort;
      [ exact IH1 | exact HS | exact H1 | exact H2 | exact IH2 | exact HS | exact HB | exact HM ].
  - split; eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_mod; try eassumption.
    exact (gunit_scoped_rel_sub (gsub_valid HG) HUs).
  - pose proof (rel_exp_under_ctx_gsub HG HB) as H1; cbn in H1.
    pose proof (rel_exp_under_ctx_gsub HG HM) as H2.
    split; eapply rel_ctx_wf_extend_dm; try eassumption.
    exact (gunit_scoped_rel_sub (gsub_valid HG) HUs).
Qed.

Generalizing over a Telescope

A type of a universe, or a proposition, generalized over a telescope of assumptions of any kinds: a Π into a type of a universe, or a ∀ into a proposition. As ctx_pi_at_wf, not into a type of levels, whose Πs take levels only (pi_kinds).
Lemma valid_exp_under_ctx_ctx_pi_at : forall r Ψ D X i, r <> ak_level -> all_assumptions Ψ -> ⊨ Ψ ++ D ->
    Ψ ++ D ⊨ X : abs_kind_sort r Typeω@i ->
    exists j, D ⊨ ctx_pi_at r Ψ X : abs_kind_sort r Typeω@j.
Proof.
  induction Ψ as [| e Ψ IH]; intros * Hr HΨa HΨ HX; cbn in *; [ eauto |].
  inversion HΨa as [| ? ? (κ & B & ->) HΨa']; subst.
  destruct (rel_ctx_wf_ass_type HΨ) as [k HB].
  pose proof (rel_ctx_wf_ass_kind HΨ) as HBk.
  eapply IH; [ exact Hr | exact HΨa' | exact (rel_ctx_wf_tail HΨ) |].
  destruct r; cbn [abs_kind_sort] in *; [| | contradiction ].
  - apply (rel_exp_under_ctx_pi_cong (i := max i k));
      [ apply HB; lia | apply HB; lia | exact HBk | eapply rel_exp_cumu_ge; [| exact HX ]; lia ].
  - exact (rel_exp_under_ctx_all (HB k (le_n k)) (HB k (le_n k)) HX).
Qed.

Corollary valid_exp_under_ctx_ctx_pi : forall Ψ D X i, all_assumptions Ψ -> ⊨ Ψ ++ D -> Ψ ++ D ⊨ X : Typeω@i ->
    exists j, D ⊨ ctx_pi Ψ X : Typeω@j.
Proof. intros * HΨa HΨ HX; exact (valid_exp_under_ctx_ctx_pi_at ak_relevant _ _ _ _ ltac:(discriminate) HΨa HΨ HX). Qed.
