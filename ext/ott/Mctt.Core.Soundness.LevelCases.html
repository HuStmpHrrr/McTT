The Rules of Levels

The rules of the types of levels in the gluing model: Level@n, the literals, succl, maxl, their congruences and the equations of levels. The lemmas they rest on are in LogicalRelation/Levels.v.

Lemma glu_level :
  `( ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ Level@n : LevelSort ).
Proof. glu_inst_intro; apply glu_rel_exp_level; glu_inst_premise. Qed.

Lemma glu_llit :
  `( fst o <= n ->
     ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ 𝕃ᵒ o : Level@n ).
Proof. glu_inst_intro; apply glu_rel_exp_llit; glu_inst_premise. Qed.

Lemma glu_succl :
  `( Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ M : Level@n ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ succl M : Level@n ).
Proof. glu_inst_intro; eapply glu_rel_exp_succl; glu_inst_premise. Qed.

Lemma glu_maxl :
  `( Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ M : Level@n ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ N : Level@n ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ maxl M N : Level@n ).
Proof. glu_inst_intro; eapply glu_rel_exp_maxl; glu_inst_premise. Qed.
