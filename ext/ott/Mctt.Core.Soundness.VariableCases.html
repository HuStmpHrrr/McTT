The Rules of Variables

The rules of variables in the gluing model: a context's lookup and the variable rules. The lemmas they rest on are in LogicalRelation/Variables.v and LogicalRelation/Instance.v.

#[local] Generalizable All Variables.

A variable at an instance is the instance's component, glued at its type at every renaming, so at the identity.
Lemma glu_vlookup :
  `( ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ->
     Γ ∋ #x : A ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊩ #x : A ).
Proof.
  glu_intro; econstructor; [ eassumption |].
  intros Γ' τ Hτ.
  apply glu_rel_exp_of_under_renaming; [ apply Hτ | apply Hτ; eassumption ].
Qed.

Lemma glu_exp_eq_var :
  `( ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ->
     Γ ∋ #x : A ->
     ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ).
Proof. glu_ctx_case. Qed.

Lemma glu_exp_eq_var_delta :
  `( ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ->
     Γ ∋ #x ≔ M : A ->
     ⊩ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ).
Proof. glu_ctx_case. Qed.
