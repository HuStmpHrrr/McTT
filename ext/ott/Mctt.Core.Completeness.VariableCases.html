Fundamental Theorem: Variables

Substitution into a variable computes (sb_extend's two clauses and exp_sub_shift_extend), so only the variable rule itself is validated here, together with the semantic weakening lemma its induction step is an instance of: #(S x) is (#x)[↑]ʷ, and the type it reports is A[↑]ʷ.
Both rest on two instantiations of a judgment about Γ: along Wk ⨟ σ at Γ', whose outer values are the goal's outer values (exp_sub_shift), and along Wk at the substituted environments, whose outer values are the goal's inner values (exp_sub_of_shift). The two share their inner values, since both evaluate the unweakened expression in ρσ ↯ and ρ'σ' ↯, and as evaluation is functional this shared pair merges the two chains into the one the goal asks for. ⟦σ[↑]ʷ⟧ρ and ⟦σ⟧(ρ↯) are different environments, which is why the bridge is a merge and not a rewrite.

Semantic Weakening

A judgment of a context still holds when read in an extension of it, with everything weakened. This is the form the induction step of valid_exp_under_ctx_vlookup needs.
The premise is the context PER of the extension, not its rel_ctx_wf: the extension is only needed for the Wk instantiation, and rel_sub_shift factors through rel_wk_shift, which asks only for the two PERs, the tail's coming from M's relation. This lets the η-rule use the lemma, since no premise of that rule mentions Γ and so ⊨ Γ is not available there.

Lemma rel_exp_under_ctx_shift : forall {Γ e A M M' env_relΓC},

  eexists_rel_exp_with i.
  intros Γ' env_rel' HΓ' σ σ' Hσj ρ ρ' ρσ ρ'σ' Hρ Hev Hev'.
The substituted environments are related in Γ ▹ C — needed to instantiate along Wk at them.
  assert (Hρσ : Dom ρσ ≈ ρ'σ' ∈ env_relΓC)
    by (eapply rel_sub_under_ctx_at'; eassumption).

  destruct (HMgen _ _ HΓ' _ _ (rel_sub_under_ctx_shift Hσj) _ _ _ _ Hρ

    [ apply (rel_exp_or_prop_evaluated Htyp1 (ex_intro _ v2 (conj Hv2 Er))) in Hexp1;

      destruct Hexp1 as [m1 m2 m3 m4 Hm1 Hm2 Hm3 Hm4 Hmchain];

    | clear Hexp1 Hexp2 ];
    clear Htyp1 Htyp2;

    rewrite <- exp_sub_shift in Hv1, Hv4;
    rewrite exp_sub_of_shift in Hu1, Hu4;

    handle_per_univ_elem_irrel;
    assert (Hmid1 : DF v2 ≈ v3 ∈ per_univ_elem i ↘ R1) by pairwise;
    assert (Hmid2 : DF v2 ≈ v3 ∈ per_univ_elem i ↘ R2) by pairwise;

    exists R1; split;
    try solve [ apply (mk_rel_exp v1 u1 u4 v4); try eassumption; merge_rel_chain Hvchain Huchain v2 ].
  - rewrite <- exp_sub_shift in Hm1, Hm4.
    rewrite exp_sub_of_shift in Hn1, Hn4.
    right; apply (mk_rel_exp m1 n1 n4 m4); try eassumption.
    merge_rel_chain Hmchain Hnchain m2.
  - left; exists v1; split; [ exact Hv1 |].
    assert (H12 : DF v1 ≈ v2 ∈ per_univ_elem i ↘ R1) by pairwise.
    pose proof (per_univ_elem_kind _ _ _ _ H12); congruence.

The Variable Case

The head of an assumption entry is read off the head clause of its context PER. The head of a definition entry is a head of the assumption entry of the same type, by restriction. A lookup one entry deeper is a weakened lookup, so the induction step is rel_exp_under_ctx_shift.
Γ ▹ A ∋ #0 : A[↑]ʷ. The type is weakened, so its four values come from the weakening lemma applied to A's judgment. The term's values are all ρσ 0 and ρ'σ' 0, given by the head clause of the context PER of Γ ▹ A. That clause speaks of the values of A in the tails, so the Wk instance of A's judgment is used a second time, to bridge those values to the values of A[↑]ʷ.
Lemma valid_exp_under_ctx_var_here_any_entry : forall {Γ e A S R},
    ctx_entry_type (e :: nil) 0 = Some A ->
    EF (e :: Γ)%list ≈ (e :: Γ)%list ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ R ->
    type_sort S ->
    Γ ⊨ A : S ->
    (e :: Γ)%list ⊨ #0 : A[↑]ʷ.
Proof.
  intros * He HR HS HA.
The type is of either sort; its judgment as a type, and that of its weakening, are read at levels of their own.
  destruct (rel_type_of_sort HS HA) as [k Hk].
  pose proof (rel_exp_under_ctx_shift HR HA) as HAwk.
  rewrite (type_sort_wk _ HS) in HAwk.
  destruct (rel_type_of_sort HS HAwk) as [i Hk'].
  pose proof (rel_type_inversion Hk) as [env_relΓ [HΓ HAgen]].
  pose proof (rel_type_inversion_simple Hk) as [env_relΓ2 [HΓ2 HAs]].
  pose proof (rel_type_inversion Hk') as [R' [HR' HAwkgen]].
  pose proof (per_ctx_env_head_split He HR) as [T [HT Hsplit]].
  assert (E2 : T <~> env_relΓ2) by (eapply per_ctx_env_right_irrel; eassumption).
  assert (E3 : R <~> R') by (eapply per_ctx_env_right_irrel; eassumption).
  refine (mk_rel_exp_under_ctx _ _ _ _ R HR i _).
  intros Γ' env_rel' HΓ' σ σ' Hσj ρ ρ' ρσ ρ'σ' Hρ Hev Hev'.
  assert (Hρσ : Dom ρσ ≈ ρ'σ' ∈ R)
    by (eapply rel_sub_under_ctx_at'; eassumption).
The type's four values, and the Wk-instantiation that bridges them to the values of A in the tails, which the head PER is stated at.
  destruct (HAwkgen _ _ HΓ' _ _ Hσj _ _ _ _ Hρ Hev Hev')
    as [v1 v2 v3 v4 Hv1 Hv2 Hv3 Hv4 Hvchain].
  destruct (eval_sub_shift_rel HR Hρσ) as [Hs Hs'].
  assert (Htl : Dom ρσ↯ ≈ ρ'σ'↯ ∈ env_relΓ).
  { pose proof (rel_wk_shift HΓ HR) as Hsh; rewrite <- !eval_wk_shift; exact (Hsh _ _ Hρσ). }
  destruct (rel_type_body_of_retagged HΓ HAgen _ _ HR _ _ (rel_sub_of_wk_rel HR HΓ (rel_wk_shift HΓ HR) (fun x Hx => ltac:(cbn; lia)))
              _ _ _ _ Hρσ Hs Hs' Htl)
    as [u1 u2 u3 u4 Hu1 Hu2 Hu3 Hu4 Huchain].
  rewrite exp_sub_of_shift in Hu1, Hu4.
  destruct (Hsplit _ _ Hρσ) as [Hteq Hhead].
  apply E2 in Hteq.
The variable is a proof exactly when A is a proposition in the tails; then so is the weakened type, and nothing is asked of the variable.
  functionalize_per_univ_chain Hvchain Rv.
  functionalize_per_univ_chain Huchain Ru.
  assert (Evu : v2 = u1) by (eapply functional_eval_exp; eassumption).
  subst u1.
  assert (Hu23 : DF u2 ≈ u3 ∈ per_univ_elem k ↘ Ru) by pairwise.
  assert (Hv12 : DF v1 ≈ v2 ∈ per_univ_elem i ↘ Rv) by pairwise.
  assert (Hv22 : DF v2 ≈ u2 ∈ per_univ_elem k ↘ Ru) by pairwise.
  pose proof (per_univ_elem_kind _ _ _ _ Hu23) as Eu23.
  pose proof (per_univ_elem_kind _ _ _ _ Hv12) as Ev12.
  pose proof (per_univ_elem_kind _ _ _ _ Hv22) as Ev22.
  pose proof (head_propositional_iff HΓ2 He HAs Hteq Hu2) as I2.
  pose proof (head_propositional_iff_right HΓ2 He HAs Hteq Hu3) as I3.
  exists Rv; split.
  { apply (mk_rel_exp v1 v2 v3 v4); eassumption. }
  destruct (evaluated_kind_or_propositional (domain_kind u2)) as [Er2 | Er2].
  2:{ left; exists v1; split; [ exact Hv1 | congruence ]. }
  right.
  assert (Hnp : ~ entry_propositional gc_deps gc_stack (e :: Γ)%list ρσ 0)
    by (rewrite entry_propositional_zero_head, I2; intro; kind_congruence).
  assert (Hnp' : ~ entry_propositional gc_deps gc_stack (e :: Γ)%list ρ'σ' 0)
    by (rewrite entry_propositional_zero_head, I3; intro; kind_congruence).
The heads, related at the PER of A in the tails, which is that of the weakened type.
  assert (Hh : Dom ρσ 0 ≈ ρ'σ' 0 ∈ Ru) by (apply (Hhead k Ru u2 u3 Hu2 Hu3 Hu23)).
  assert (Hv23 : DF v2 ≈ v3 ∈ per_univ_elem i ↘ Rv) by pairwise.
  assert (Hvu : Rv <~> Ru) by (eapply per_univ_elem_right_irrel; eassumption).
  assert (PER Rv) by (eapply per_elem_PER; eassumption).

  apply (mk_rel_exp (ρσ 0) (ρσ 0) (ρ'σ' 0) (ρ'σ' 0));
    [ exact (eval_sub_var _ _ _ _ 0 Hev ltac:(cbn; lia) Hnp) | apply eval_exp_var | apply eval_exp_var
    | exact (eval_sub_var _ _ _ _ 0 Hev' ltac:(cbn; lia) Hnp') |].
  apply rel_chain_4_of_2; [ assumption | apply Hvu; exact Hh ].
Qed.

Lemma valid_exp_under_ctx_var_here : forall {Γ κ A},
    ⊨ Γ ▹{κ} A ->
    Γ ▹{κ} A ⊨ #0 : A[↑]ʷ.
Proof.
  intros * HΓ.

Lemma valid_exp_under_ctx_vlookup : forall {Γ x A},
    Γ ∋ #x : A ->
    ⊨ Γ ->
    Γ ⊨ #x : A.
Proof.
  induction 1 as [A Γ | A M Γ | A M U Γ | x A Γ e Hx IH]; intros HΓ.
  - apply valid_exp_under_ctx_var_here; assumption.
  -
Γ ▸ A ≔ M ∋ #0 : A[↑]ʷ: its head is read as an assumption's.
    inversion HΓ as [| | ? ? ? Srt ? HΓ0 HΓAper HS HA HM | |]; subst.
    eapply valid_exp_under_ctx_var_here_any_entry; [ reflexivity | exact HΓAper | exact HS | exact HA ].
  -
Γ ▸ₘ A ≔ M ‖ U ∋ #0 : A[↑]ʷ likewise.
    inversion HΓ as [| | | | ? ? ? ? i ? HΓ0 HΓAper HA HM _ _]; subst.
    eapply valid_exp_under_ctx_var_here_any_entry; [ reflexivity | exact HΓAper | exact (type_sort_universe i) | exact HA ].

    pose proof (rel_ctx_wf_per_ctx_env HΓ) as [env_relΓe HΓeper].
    exact (rel_exp_under_ctx_shift HΓeper (IH (rel_ctx_wf_tail HΓ))).
Qed.

δ for Local Definitions

At #0 both sides are valid, so it suffices to relate their values pointwise (rel_exp_under_ctx_of_simple). The value of #0 is the head of the environment, the value of M[↑]ʷ is related to the value of M in the tail, and the tie relates those two. A deeper lookup is again a weakened one.

Lemma sem_exp_eq_var_delta :
  `( ⊨ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ->
     Γ ∋ #x ≔ M : A ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ #x ≈ M : A ).

  intros * HΓ Hx; sem_intro; [ assumption |].
  clear - Hx HΓ; revert HΓ.
  induction Hx as [A M Γ | A M U Γ | x A M Γ e Hx IH]; intros HΓ.

  - pose proof (rel_ctx_wf_per_ctx_env HΓ) as [env_relΓe HΓeper].
    exact (rel_exp_under_ctx_shift HΓeper (IH (rel_ctx_wf_tail HΓ))).
