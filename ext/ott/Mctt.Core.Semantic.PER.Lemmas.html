Mctt.Core.Semantic.PER.Lemmas

PER Lemmas

The lemmas on the PERs of the model, in seven parts, each on top of the one before:
  • Basic: the morphisms of the evaluation relations and the basic PERs (per_bot, per_top, levels, per_ne, per_prop, per_proof);
  • Universe: the universe PER is of one kind, right irrelevant, symmetric and transitive; chains of related types;
  • UniverseSubtyping: types of levels, the constructors and the inversion of the universe PER, cumulativity, per_subtyp;
  • ModuleValues: the module PER per_dmod is a PER;
  • Contexts: the heads of context PERs, per_ctx_env is a PER;
  • CanonicalContexts: the canonical context PERs;
  • Environments: extending related environments, agreement up to retagging, and the readability of kept proofs.
