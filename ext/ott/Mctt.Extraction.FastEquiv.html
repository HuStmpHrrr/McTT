Mctt.Extraction.FastEquiv

With the Skip Off, the Fast Relations Are Core's

At skip_off_mode every recursive result is computed (fast_eval_ihs_skip needs the skip on), a kept proof reads back under its definitions (fast_read_proof_kept) and a type former at no argument is looked up (fast_read_typ_indty_nil needs the skip on): the rules left are those of Core.Semantic.Evaluation and Core.Semantic.Readback, one for one. So the extracted normalizer, run with the skip off, computes the Core normal form exactly; it does so where a normal form computed with the skip on has a proof in it (Extraction.NbE).
