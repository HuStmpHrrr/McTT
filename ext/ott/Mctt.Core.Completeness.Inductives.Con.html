The Constructors of a Valid Group

A constructor of a member k of a valid group D at p is an element of its type (valid_exp_under_ctx_con): at related parameters and arguments, its value is related at the type of the member there. The type's relation is the group's canonical one at k (member_type_rel), at a view through the agreement of the view with its container (view_agreement_canonical_all); the value is a constructor of the member in it (con_rel), its recursive arguments related by their shapes (shape_char).
At a view, the constructor takes the group's uniform parameters and is then its container's constructor at the view's instance there: its values along the arguments are a relation (con_value), not a function of them, so its type is read by ctx_pi_rel_elem_by.

The Sorts of a Constructor's Arguments

Corresponding arguments of a view's and of its container's constructors are of the same kind (cargs_correspond).
Lemma cargs_correspond_tags : forall vm gs' gs j, cargs_correspond vm gs' gs ->
    match List.nth_error gs' j with Some (ca_arg r _) => r | _ => ak_relevant end =
    match List.nth_error gs j with Some (ca_arg r _) => r | _ => ak_relevant end.
Proof.
  intros vm; induction gs' as [| g' gs' IH]; intros [| g gs] j H; cbn in H; try contradiction.
  - destruct j; reflexivity.
  - destruct g'; contradiction.
  - destruct g' as [r' A' | t'], g as [r A | t]; cbn in H; try contradiction; destruct j as [| j]; cbn [List.nth_error].
    + destruct H as [-> _]; reflexivity.
    + apply IH; tauto.
    + destruct H as [-> _]; reflexivity.
    + apply IH; tauto.
    + reflexivity.
    + apply IH; tauto.
Qed.

So the two constructors' arguments have the same kinds.
Lemma cargs_correspond_kinds : forall vm gs' gs, cargs_correspond vm gs' gs -> cargs_kinds gs' = cargs_kinds gs.
Proof.
  intros vm; induction gs' as [| [r' A' | t'] gs' IH]; intros [| [r A | t] gs] H; cbn in H; try contradiction; [ reflexivity | | |];
    unfold cargs_kinds in *; cbn [List.map]; f_equal; try (apply IH; tauto); tauto.
Qed.

Lemma app_split_right : forall {X} (l1 l2 m1 m2 : list X) x, l1 ++ l2 = m1 ++ x :: m2 -> List.length m2 < List.length l2 ->
    exists m1', l2 = m1' ++ x :: m2.

Qed.

The Relation of a Member's Type

The type of the member k at the uniform parameters as variables, after more variables zs, at two related environments: its relation is the group's at k. At a declared member it is the group's canonical one directly; at a view, by the agreement of the view with its container (view_end_rel_all); at a record view, by the coherence of the record view with its record (rview_end_rel_all).
