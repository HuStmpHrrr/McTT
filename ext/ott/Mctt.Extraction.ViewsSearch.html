Mctt.Extraction.ViewsSearch

The View Search, as a Total Function

The views of a group declaration are found depth first (views_dfs, in Core.Syntactic.Inductives.Group) from the instances its constructors meet. This file computes them with no fuel, generically over the data the checker files for each view: a functional relation E x a ("the view x has the data a", from which its siblings sibs_of a and the instances its constructors meet meets_of a are read), decided by E_dec.
  • The search (search) is by structural recursion on an accessibility proof in Prop (erased by extraction), for the order "more views found, or as many and a shorter queue" (search_lt); it needs only that the keys reachable from the queue are of finitely many views, equal up to their proofs (reach_finite): the views found are of pairwise different views (key_nodup). It is sound and complete for views_dfs.
  • The finiteness itself follows from a cluster argument, up to proofs (reach_finite_of_clusters): every key of an invariant Inv has a finite cluster whose views' keys of Inv have their siblings of its views and meet instances of its views or of a finite list below it in a well-founded order lt; the meets of any view, the siblings of a view of Inv and the queue's instances are of Inv. The instances of these hypotheses, read off the PER model, are in Extraction.ViewsReach, Extraction.ViewsCluster and Extraction.ViewsFound.

  Lemma Forall2_nth : forall {X Y} (R : X -> Y -> Prop) l1 l2 v x, Forall2 R l1 l2 -> nth_error l1 v = Some x ->

    - injection Hv as <-; eauto.
    - eauto.

Keys of pairwise different views.
  Fixpoint key_nodup (l : list view_key) : Prop :=
    match l with
    | nil => True
    | x :: l' => ~ key_in x l' /\ key_nodup l'
    end.

  Lemma key_nodup_snoc : forall l s, key_nodup l -> ~ key_in s l -> key_nodup (l ++ [s]).
  Proof.
    induction l as [| x l IH]; intros s Hd Hs; cbn in *; [ split; [ intros (y & [] & _) | exact I ] |].
    destruct Hd as [Hx Hd]; split; [| apply IH; [ exact Hd | intros Hk; apply Hs; destruct Hk as (y & Hy & Hsy);
                                                   exists y; split; [ right; exact Hy | exact Hsy ] ] ].
    intros (y & Hy & Hxy); apply in_app_or in Hy as [Hy | [<- | []]]; [ apply Hx; exists y; split; assumption |].
    apply Hs; exists x; split; [ left; reflexivity | symmetry; exact Hxy ].
  Qed.

Keys of pairwise different views, each of the view of a key of L, are at most as many as L.
  Lemma key_nodup_length : forall Vs L, key_nodup Vs -> (forall y, In y Vs -> key_in y L) -> List.length Vs <= List.length L.
  Proof.
    induction Vs as [| x Vs IH]; intros L Hd HL; cbn; [ lia |].
    destruct Hd as [Hx Hd].
    destruct (HL x (or_introl eq_refl)) as (l & Hl & Hxl).
    assert (Hlt : List.length (remove view_key_eq_dec l L) < List.length L) by (apply remove_length_lt; exact Hl).
    enough (List.length Vs <= List.length (remove view_key_eq_dec l L)) by lia.
    apply IH; [ exact Hd |].
    intros y Hy; destruct (HL y (or_intror Hy)) as (l' & Hl' & Hyl').
    exists l'; split; [| exact Hyl' ].
    apply in_in_remove; [| exact Hl' ].
    intros ->; apply Hx; exists y; split; [ exact Hy | etransitivity; [ exact Hxl | symmetry; exact Hyl' ] ].
  Qed.

  Lemma views_new_nodup : forall acc ss, key_nodup acc -> key_nodup (acc ++ views_new acc ss).
  Proof.
    intros acc ss; revert acc; induction ss as [| s ss IH]; cbn; intros acc Hd; [ rewrite app_nil_r; exact Hd |].
    destruct (view_key_mem s acc) eqn:Hm; [ apply IH, Hd |].
    replace (acc ++ s :: views_new (acc ++ [s]) ss) with ((acc ++ [s]) ++ views_new (acc ++ [s]) ss)
      by (rewrite <- List.app_assoc; reflexivity).
    apply IH, key_nodup_snoc; [ exact Hd |].
    intros Hk; apply view_key_mem_spec in Hk; congruence.
  Qed.

A new view is of no view found before.
  Lemma views_new_fresh : forall acc ss y, In y (views_new acc ss) -> ~ key_in y acc.
  Proof.
    intros acc ss; revert acc; induction ss as [| s ss IH]; cbn; intros acc y H Hk; [ exact H |].
    destruct (view_key_mem s acc) eqn:Hm; [ exact (IH _ _ H Hk) |].

A view a run finds is one found before, or of no view found before.
  Lemma views_dfs_fresh : forall sibs meets acc tr Vf, views_dfs sibs meets acc tr Vf ->
      forall z, In z Vf -> In z acc \/ ~ key_in z acc.
  Proof.
    induction 1 as [acc | acc x tr Vf _ IH]; intros z Hz; [ left; exact Hz |].
    destruct (IH z Hz) as [Hi | Hn].

      destruct (view_key_mem x acc); [ destruct Hi | exact (views_new_fresh _ _ _ Hi) ].
    - right; intros (w & Hw & Hzw); apply Hn; exists w; split; [ apply in_or_app; left; exact Hw | exact Hzw ].
  Qed.

The Search's Order

More views found, or as many and a shorter queue; the views found are of pairwise different views and reachable, and the reachable keys are of finitely many views, so there are boundedly many.
The views found are of pairwise different views and reachable.
  Definition found_ok (Vs : list view_key) : Prop := key_nodup Vs /\ forall y, In y Vs -> reach y.

The order of the search's states (the views found, the queue).
  Definition search_lt (s' s : prod (list view_key) (list view_key)) : Prop :=
    found_ok (fst s') /\
    ((incl (fst s) (fst s') /\ List.length (fst s) < List.length (fst s')) \/
     (fst s' = fst s /\ List.length (snd s') < List.length (snd s))).

  Section Order.
The reachable keys are of the views of finitely many keys.
    Hypothesis reach_finite : exists L, forall y, reach y -> key_in y L.

    Lemma search_acc : forall Vs tr, Acc search_lt (Vs, tr).
    Proof.
      destruct reach_finite as [L HL].
      assert (Hb : forall Vs, found_ok Vs -> List.length Vs <= List.length L).
      { intros Vs [Hd Hr]; apply key_nodup_length; [ exact Hd | intros y Hy; apply HL, Hr, Hy ]. }
      assert (H : forall n m Vs tr, List.length L - List.length Vs <= n -> List.length tr <= m -> Acc search_lt (Vs, tr)).
      { induction n as [n IHn] using (well_founded_induction lt_wf); induction m as [m IHm] using (well_founded_induction lt_wf).
        intros Vs tr Hn Hm; constructor; intros [Vs' tr'] Hl; unfold search_lt in Hl; cbn in Hl;
          destruct Hl as [Hok [[Hi Hlt] | [-> Hlt]]].
        - pose proof (Hb _ Hok); apply (IHn (List.length L - List.length Vs') ltac:(lia) (List.length tr') Vs' tr'); lia.
        - apply (IHm (List.length tr') ltac:(lia) _ _ Hn); lia. }
      intros; eapply H; reflexivity.
    Qed.
  End Order.

The data of a list of views, or the first one that has none, with why; the data ak of the view k is known already.
  Fixpoint data_of (k : view_key) (ak : A) (Hk : E k ak) (ys : list view_key) :
      ({As | Forall2 E ys As} + {ye : view_key * Err | In (fst ye) ys /\ forall a, ~ E (fst ye) a})%type.
  Proof.
    destruct ys as [| y ys].
    - left; exists nil; constructor.
    - assert (Hy : ({a | E y a} + {e : Err | forall a, ~ E y a})%type).
      { destruct (view_key_eq_dec y k) as [-> | _]; [ left; exists ak; exact Hk | exact (E_dec y) ]. }
      destruct Hy as [[a Ha] | [e Hn]]; [| right; exists (y, e); split; [ left; reflexivity | exact Hn ] ].
      destruct (data_of k ak Hk ys) as [[As HAs] | [ye [Hy Hn]]]; [ left; exists (a :: As); constructor; assumption |].
      right; exists ye; split; [ right; exact Hy | exact Hn ].
  Defined.

What the search returns from the views Vs found and the queue tr: the views Vf (with their data Af) of a run of views_dfs at the tables of the data, or a view with no data (with why) and a refutation of every run whose tables agree with E and whose views all have data.
  Definition result (Vs tr : list view_key) : Type :=
    ({p : prod (list view_key) (list A) |
      key_nodup (fst p) /\ Forall2 E (fst p) (snd p) /\
      views_dfs (siblings_table (fst p) (List.map sibs_of (snd p))) (met_table (fst p) (List.map meets_of (snd p))) Vs tr (fst p)}
    + {ye : view_key * Err | forall sibs meets Vf, views_dfs sibs meets Vs tr Vf ->
        (forall y a, In y Vf -> E y a -> sibs y = sibs_of a /\ meets y = meets_of a) ->
        (forall y, ~ In y Vf -> sibs y = y :: nil) ->
        (forall y, In y Vf -> exists a, E y a) -> False})%type.

  Lemma views_met_new : forall sibs Vs x, ~ key_in x Vs -> views_met sibs Vs x = views_new Vs (sibs x ++ [x]).
  Proof.
    intros * Hx; unfold views_met; destruct (view_key_mem x Vs) eqn:Hm; [| reflexivity ].
    exfalso; apply Hx, view_key_mem_spec, Hm.
  Qed.

  Lemma views_met_old : forall sibs Vs x, key_in x Vs -> views_met sibs Vs x = nil.
  Proof. intros * Hx; unfold views_met; apply view_key_mem_spec in Hx; rewrite Hx; reflexivity. Qed.

An instance met first is a view the run finds itself: else it has no siblings, and is the one view it adds.
  Lemma met_found : forall sibs meets Vs x tr Vf, views_dfs sibs meets (Vs ++ views_met sibs Vs x) tr Vf ->
      ~ key_in x Vs -> (forall y, ~ In y Vf -> sibs y = y :: nil) -> In x Vf.
  Proof.
    intros * Hb Hx Habs; destruct (in_dec view_key_eq_dec x Vf) as [Hi | Hi]; [ exact Hi |].
    apply (views_dfs_incl _ _ _ _ _ Hb), in_or_app; right.
    rewrite views_met_new, (Habs x Hi) by exact Hx; cbn.
    destruct (view_key_mem x Vs) eqn:Hm; [ exfalso; apply Hx, view_key_mem_spec, Hm | left; reflexivity ].
  Qed.

  Lemma map_table : forall Vf Af ys bs, key_nodup Vf -> Forall2 E Vf Af -> incl ys Vf -> Forall2 E ys bs ->
      List.map (met_table Vf (List.map meets_of Af)) ys = List.map meets_of bs.
  Proof.
    intros * Hd HF Hi Hy; induction Hy as [| y b ys bs Hb Hys IH]; cbn; [ reflexivity |].
    f_equal; [ apply (table_at _ _ _ _ HF (Hi y (or_introl eq_refl)) Hb) | apply IH; intros z Hz; apply Hi; right; exact Hz ].
  Qed.

The search, by recursion on the accessibility proof.
  Fixpoint search (Vs : list view_key) (As : list A) (tr : list view_key)
      (Hd : key_nodup Vs) (HF : Forall2 E Vs As) (Hr : forall y, In y Vs -> reach y)
      (Ht : forall y, In y tr -> reach y) (Hacc : Acc search_lt (Vs, tr)) {struct Hacc} : result Vs tr.
  Proof.
    destruct Hacc as [Hacc].
    destruct tr as [| x tr'].
    { left; exists (Vs, As); cbn; split; [ exact Hd | split; [ exact HF | constructor ] ]. }
    destruct (view_key_mem x Vs) eqn:Hmem; [ apply view_key_mem_spec in Hmem as Hx | assert (Hx : ~ key_in x Vs) by
                                               (intros Hk; apply view_key_mem_spec in Hk; congruence) ].
    - (* of a view found already: nothing found, the queue shrinks *)
      refine (match search Vs As tr' Hd HF Hr (fun y Hy => Ht y (or_intror Hy)) (Hacc (Vs, tr') _) with
              | inl p => inl _ | inr Hf => inr _ end).
      + split; [ split; assumption | right; cbn; split; [ reflexivity | lia ] ].
      + destruct p as [[Vf Af] (Hd' & HF' & Hb)]; exists (Vf, Af); cbn in *; split; [ exact Hd' | split; [ exact HF' |] ].
        apply views_dfs_step; rewrite views_met_old by exact Hx; cbn; rewrite !app_nil_r; exact Hb.
      + destruct Hf as [ye Hf]; exists ye.
        intros sibs meets Vf Hb Hag Habs Hex; inversion Hb; subst.
        rewrite views_met_old in * by exact Hx; cbn in *; rewrite !app_nil_r in *.
        eapply Hf; eassumption.
    - destruct (E_dec x) as [[ax Hax] | [e Hn]].
      2:{ right; exists (x, e); intros sibs meets Vf Hb Hag Habs Hex; inversion Hb; subst.
          destruct (Hex x) as [a Ha]; [ eapply met_found; eassumption | exact (Hn a Ha) ]. }
      remember (views_new Vs (sibs_of ax ++ [x])) as nw eqn:Hnw.
      destruct (in_dec view_key_eq_dec x nw) as [Hxnew | Hxnew].
      2:{
A sibling listed before x is of its view: no run finds x
          right; exists (x, shadowed_err); intros sibs meets Vf Hb Hag Habs Hex; inversion Hb; subst.
          assert (HxVf : In x Vf) by (eapply met_found; eassumption).
          rewrite views_met_new, (proj1 (Hag x ax HxVf Hax)) in * by exact Hx.
          match goal with H : views_dfs _ _ (Vs ++ views_new _ _) _ Vf |- _ => rename H into Hb' end.
          destruct (views_dfs_fresh _ _ _ _ _ Hb' x HxVf) as [Hi | Hn].
          - apply in_app_or in Hi as [Hi | Hi]; [ exact (Hx (key_in_of_in _ _ Hi)) | exact (Hxnew Hi) ].
          - apply Hn; destruct (views_new_in Vs (sibs_of ax ++ [x]) x ltac:(apply in_or_app; right; left; reflexivity))
              as [(w & Hw & Hxw) | (w & Hw & Hxw)]; [ exfalso; apply Hx; exists w; split; assumption |].
            exists w; split; [ apply in_or_app; right; exact Hw | exact Hxw ]. }
      destruct (data_of x ax Hax nw) as [[bs Hbs] | [ye [Hy Hny]]].
      2:{ right; exists ye; intros sibs meets Vf Hb Hag Habs Hex; inversion Hb; subst.
          assert (HxVf : In x Vf) by (eapply met_found; eassumption).
          rewrite views_met_new, (proj1 (Hag x ax HxVf Hax)) in * by exact Hx.
          destruct (Hex (fst ye)) as [b Hb']; [| exact (Hny b Hb') ].
          eapply views_dfs_incl; [ eassumption | apply in_or_app; right; exact Hy ]. }
      assert (Hrnew : forall y, In y nw -> reach y)
        by (intros y Hy; rewrite Hnw in Hy; apply views_new_incl in Hy;
            eapply reach_sibling; [ apply Ht; left; reflexivity | exact Hax | exact Hy ]).
      assert (Hok : found_ok (Vs ++ nw)).
      { split; [ rewrite Hnw; apply views_new_nodup, Hd |].
        intros y Hy; apply in_app_or in Hy as [Hy | Hy]; auto. }
      assert (Htr : forall y, In y (List.concat (List.map meets_of bs) ++ tr') -> reach y).
      { intros y Hy; apply in_app_or in Hy as [Hy | Hy]; [| apply Ht; right; exact Hy ].
        apply in_concat in Hy as (ms & Hms & Hy); apply in_map_iff in Hms as (b & <- & Hb).
        destruct (In_nth_error _ _ Hb) as [v Hv].
        destruct (Forall2_nth _ _ _ _ _ (Forall2_flip Hbs) Hv) as (z & Hz & Hzb).
        eapply reach_meet; [ apply Hrnew, (nth_error_In _ _ Hz) | exact Hzb | exact Hy ]. }
      assert (Hlt : search_lt (Vs ++ nw, List.concat (List.map meets_of bs) ++ tr') (Vs, x :: tr')).
      { split; [ exact Hok | left; cbn; split; [ intros y Hy; apply in_or_app; left; exact Hy |] ].
        rewrite length_app; destruct nw; [ destruct Hxnew | cbn; lia ]. }
      refine (match search (Vs ++ nw) (As ++ bs) (List.concat (List.map meets_of bs) ++ tr')
                (proj1 Hok) (Forall2_app HF Hbs) (proj2 Hok) Htr (Hacc _ Hlt) with
              | inl p => inl _ | inr Hf => inr _ end).
      + destruct p as [[Vf Af] (Hd' & HF' & Hb)]; exists (Vf, Af); cbn in *; split; [ exact Hd' | split; [ exact HF' |] ].
        assert (Hinc : incl (Vs ++ nw) Vf) by (intros y Hy; eapply views_dfs_incl; eassumption).
        apply views_dfs_step; rewrite views_met_new by exact Hx.
        rewrite (proj1 (table_at _ _ _ _ HF' (Hinc x ltac:(apply in_or_app; right; exact Hxnew)) Hax)).
        rewrite <- Hnw, (map_table _ _ _ _ Hd' HF' (fun y Hy => Hinc y (in_or_app _ _ _ (or_intror Hy))) Hbs); exact Hb.
      + destruct Hf as [ye Hf]; exists ye.
        intros sibs meets Vf Hb Hag Habs Hex; inversion Hb; subst.
        assert (HxVf : In x Vf) by (eapply met_found; eassumption).
        rewrite views_met_new, (proj1 (Hag x ax HxVf Hax)) in * by exact Hx.
        assert (Hm : List.map meets (views_new Vs (sibs_of ax ++ [x])) = List.map meets_of bs).
        { assert (Hi : incl (views_new Vs (sibs_of ax ++ [x])) Vf)
            by (intros y Hy; eapply views_dfs_incl; [ eassumption | apply in_or_app; right; exact Hy ]).
          clear - Hbs Hag Hi; revert Hi; induction Hbs as [| y b ys bs' Hyb _ IH]; intros Hi; cbn; [ reflexivity |].
          f_equal; [ exact (proj2 (Hag y b (Hi y (or_introl eq_refl)) Hyb)) | apply IH; intros z Hz; apply Hi; right; exact Hz ]. }
        rewrite Hm in *; eapply Hf; eassumption.
  Defined.

The search from no view found and the declared members' instances, given that the reachable keys are of finitely many views.
  Definition views_search_from (Hfin : exists L, forall y, reach y -> key_in y L) : result nil init :=
    search nil nil init I (Forall2_nil _) (fun y H => match H with end)
      (fun y H => reach_init y H) (search_acc Hfin nil init).

The order the meets leaving a cluster descend.

L is a cluster of the view x, and M the instances its views meet outside it, up to proofs: every key of the invariant of the view of one of L has its siblings of the views of L, and meets instances of the views of L or of M; those of M are of the invariant and below x.
    Record cluster_of (x : view_key) (L M : list view_key) : Prop := mk_cluster_of
      {
It holds x
        cluster_self : In x L
        
It holds the siblings of its views, up to proofs
      ; cluster_closed : forall y ay w, Inv y -> key_in y L -> E y ay -> In w (sibs_of ay) -> key_in w L
        
The instances its views meet are of its views or of M
      ; cluster_meets : forall y ay z, Inv y -> key_in y L -> E y ay -> In z (meets_of ay) -> key_in z L \/ key_in z M
        
Those of M are of the invariant, and below x
      ; cluster_below : forall z, In z M -> Inv z /\ lt z x }.

The keys reachable from the keys of the invariant of the view of x are of the views of finitely many keys.
    Definition finite_cls (x : view_key) : Prop :=
      exists L, forall x', Inv x' -> view_key_eq x' x -> forall v, reach_from x' v -> key_in v L.

One list bounds the reachable keys of the views of a list's keys, each with such a bound.
    Lemma cover_cls : forall ys, (forall y, In y ys -> finite_cls y) ->
        exists L, forall y, In y ys -> forall x', Inv x' -> view_key_eq x' y -> forall v, reach_from x' v -> key_in v L.
    Proof.
      induction ys as [| y ys IH]; intros H; [ exists nil; intros ? [] |].
      destruct IH as [L HL]; [ intros; apply H; right; assumption |].
      destruct (H y (or_introl eq_refl)) as [Ly HLy].
      exists (Ly ++ L); intros y' [<- | Hin] x' Hx' Hxy v Hv; [ apply key_in_app_l; exact (HLy _ Hx' Hxy _ Hv) |].
      apply key_in_app_r; exact (HL _ Hin _ Hx' Hxy _ Hv).
    Qed.

From the keys of the invariant of the view of a key of the invariant, the reachable keys are of finitely many views: those of its cluster, and those reachable from the meets below it.
    Lemma finite_of_inv : forall x, Inv x -> finite_cls x.
    Proof.
      intros x Hx; pose proof (lt_acc x Hx) as Hacc; revert Hx; induction Hacc as [x _ IH]; intros Hx.
      destruct (cluster x Hx) as (L & M & HL).
      destruct (cover_cls M) as [LM HLM].
      { intros z Hz; destruct (cluster_below _ _ _ HL _ Hz) as [Hiz Hlt]; exact (IH _ Hlt Hiz). }
      exists (L ++ LM); intros x' Hx' Hxx' v Hv.
      assert (Hcl : (Inv v /\ key_in v L) \/ exists z w, In z M /\ Inv w /\ view_key_eq w z /\ reach_from w v).
      { induction Hv as [| y a w Hy IHy Ha Hw | y a w Hy IHy Ha Hw].
        - left; split; [ exact Hx' |]; exists x; split; [ exact (cluster_self _ _ _ HL) | exact Hxx' ].
        - destruct IHy as [[Hiy HyL] | (z & u & Hz & Hu & Huz & Huy)].
          + apply in_app_or in Hw as [Hw | [<- | []]]; [| left; split; assumption ].
            left; split; [ exact (sibs_inv _ _ _ Hiy Ha Hw) | exact (cluster_closed _ _ _ HL _ _ _ Hiy HyL Ha Hw) ].
          + right; exists z, u; repeat split; try assumption; eapply reach_from_sibling; eassumption.
        - destruct IHy as [[Hiy HyL] | (z & u & Hz & Hu & Huz & Huy)].
          + pose proof (meets_inv _ _ _ Ha Hw) as Hiw.
            destruct (cluster_meets _ _ _ HL _ _ _ Hiy HyL Ha Hw) as [HwL | (z & Hz & Hwz)]; [ left; split; assumption |].
            right; exists z, w; repeat split; [ exact Hz | exact Hiw | exact Hwz | apply reach_from_self ].
          + right; exists z, u; repeat split; try assumption; eapply reach_from_meet; eassumption. }
      destruct Hcl as [[_ HvL] | (z & u & Hz & Hu & Huz & Huv)]; [ apply key_in_app_l; exact HvL |].
      apply key_in_app_r; exact (HLM _ Hz _ Hu Huz _ Huv).
    Qed.

    Lemma reach_from_init : forall y, reach y -> exists x, In x init /\ reach_from x y.
    Proof.
      induction 1 as [x Hx | x a y Hx IH Ha Hy | x a y Hx IH Ha Hy].
      - exists x; split; [ exact Hx | apply reach_from_self ].
      - destruct IH as (x0 & Hx0 & Hr); exists x0; split; [ exact Hx0 | eapply reach_from_sibling; eassumption ].
      - destruct IH as (x0 & Hx0 & Hr); exists x0; split; [ exact Hx0 | eapply reach_from_meet; eassumption ].
    Qed.

So the keys reachable from the queue are of finitely many views.
    Theorem reach_finite_of_clusters : exists L, forall y, reach y -> key_in y L.
    Proof.
      destruct (cover_cls init) as [L HL]; [ intros y Hy; exact (finite_of_inv _ (init_inv _ Hy)) |].
      exists L; intros y Hy; destruct (reach_from_init _ Hy) as (x & Hx & Hr).
      exact (HL _ Hx _ (init_inv _ Hx) ltac:(reflexivity) _ Hr).
    Qed.
