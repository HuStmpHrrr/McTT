Generalizable All Variables.

That a normal form is the type of a type: a universe, whose types are relevant, Prop, whose types are propositions, or LevelSort, whose types are types of levels; κ is the kind of the types it sorts (sort_kind). The side condition of every algorithmic rule that asks for a type of some sort. The universe u is the one the type is counted in when a Π joins its parts: a proposition and a type of levels count as the least small universe, as a domain of another kind does not bound the universe of a Π (wf_pi).
Variant is_sort_nf : nf -> abs_kind -> universe_nf -> Prop :=
A universe: its types are relevant
| is_sort_nf_univ : forall W u, is_univ_nf W u -> is_sort_nf W ak_relevant u
Prop: its types are propositions
| is_sort_nf_prop : is_sort_nf Propⁿ ak_propositional (universe_small (ordinal_of_nat 0, nil))
LevelSort: its types are types of levels
| is_sort_nf_level : is_sort_nf LevelSortⁿ ak_level (universe_small (ordinal_of_nat 0, nil)).

A normal form is the type of a type of one sort and universe.
Lemma is_sort_nf_functional : forall W r r' u u', is_sort_nf W r u -> is_sort_nf W r' u' -> r = r' /\ u = u'.
Proof.
  intros * H H'; destruct H as [W u Hu | |]; inversion H'; subst;
    try solve [ inversion Hu ]; try solve [ match goal with Hu' : is_univ_nf _ _ |- _ => inversion Hu' end ];
    [ split; [ reflexivity | eapply is_univ_nf_functional; eassumption ] | split; reflexivity | split; reflexivity ].
Qed.

nfs_agree Γ Ms Rs Ts: the terms Ms and Rs have, position by position, normal forms equal up to proofs at the types Ts. The eliminator's rule compares by it the uniform parameters its member's type has at the eliminator's uniform parameters with those of the type its scrutinee infers.
Inductive nfs_agree (Γ : ctx) : list exp -> list exp -> list typ -> Prop :=
No term
| nfs_agree_nil : nfs_agree Γ nil nil nil
The first terms have the normal forms W and W', equal up to proofs, at the first type, then the rest agree
| nfs_agree_cons : forall M R T W W' Ms Rs Ts,
    nbe_f Γ M T W ->
    nbe_f Γ R T W' ->
    nf_eq_upto_proofs W W' ->
    nfs_agree Γ Ms Rs Ts ->
    nfs_agree Γ (M :: Ms) (R :: Rs) (T :: Ts).

alg_ty_conv Γ A B: the types A and B have normal forms equal up to proofs, as completeness gives equal types.
Definition alg_ty_conv (Γ : ctx) (A B : typ) : Prop :=
  exists W W', nbe_ty_f Γ A W /\ nbe_ty_f Γ B W' /\ nf_eq_upto_proofs W W'.

nbe_ty_kind Γ A κ: the type A is of the kind κ, read off its value at the initial environment (domain_kind): a type of a universe has a relevant value, a proposition a propositional one, a type of levels one of levels. It decides the sort of a type the algorithm has inferred, which is a normal form, not a subterm, so it is not inferred again.
Definition nbe_ty_kind (Γ : ctx) (A : typ) (κ : abs_kind) : Prop :=
  exists ρ a, initial_env_f Γ ρ /\ ⟦ A ⟧ ρ ↘ a /\ domain_kind a = κ.

A Π is at the join of the universes of its parts (universe_nf_pi_tm): a large universe absorbs a small one, and two small ones join on levels, the codomain's bounded under the binder (lvl_bound): an atom that mentions the bound variable is bounded by its sort. The join is the normal form of that universe, a side condition rather than the conclusion's index: an index computed from the premises makes the judgment's inversion non-terminating. The side conditions on the parts are is_univ_nf rather than equations on a deciding function, for the reason given there. The codomain's universe is taken normalised (UB'): the bound trusts the sorts its atoms carry, and those of a normal form are the sorts of the atoms' types. (An inferred type is already normal, alg_type_infer_normal, so UB' is UB; the premise is what lets soundness, which that theorem rests on, see it.) One rule for every Π, as wf_pi: the domain is a type of the kind κd the Π carries, the codomain one of the kind κc, at kinds a Π may join (pi_kinds), and the Π is of the sort of the kind κc at that join (abs_kind_sort): a type of functions in the join, a universal proposition in Prop, a type of functions between levels in LevelSort. A proposition and a type of levels count as the least small universe (is_sort_nf).

Lemma user_exp_all : forall M, user_exp M.
Proof.
  eapply proj1, (syn_mut_ind user_exp (fun _ => True)
    (fun b => match b with b_def A M | b_def_mod A M _ => user_exp A /\ user_exp M | b_mod _ => True end)
    (fun _ => True) (fun _ => True) (fun _ => True) (fun _ => True) (fun _ => True));
    intros; try destruct_conjs; try match goal with b : binding |- _ => destruct b; cbn in *; destruct_conjs end;
    try lazymatch goal with
      | HU : List.Forall user_exp ?Us, HB : List.Forall _ ?Bks |- user_exp (a_elim _ _ ?Us _ ?Bks _) =>
          rewrite List.Forall_forall in HU, HB;
          constructor; [ exact HU
                       | intros b Hb; exact (proj1 (HB b Hb))
                       | intros b Hb; exact (proj1 (proj2 (HB b Hb)))
                       | intros b br Hb Hbr; exact (proj1 (List.Forall_forall _ _) (proj2 (proj2 (HB b Hb))) br Hbr)
                       | assumption ]
      | HF : List.Forall _ ?fs |- user_exp (a_tuple _ ?fs) =>
          rewrite List.Forall_forall in HF; constructor; [ assumption | exact HF ]
      end;
    mauto 3.
Qed.
