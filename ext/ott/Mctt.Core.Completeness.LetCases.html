The Rules of Lets

The rules of the three lets in the PER model: of a term, of a module and of a definition with a module part, their congruences and their ζ-rules. The lemmas they rest on are in LogicalRelation/Lets.v and, for the module lets, Modules/Slots.v.

Lemma sem_exp_eq_let_zeta :
  `( Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ A : Srt ->
     type_sort Srt ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ M : A ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▸ A ≔ M ⊨ B : C ->
     exp_scoped (List.length Γ) M ->
     Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊨ ℓ A ≔ M in B ≈ B[Id,,M] : C[Id,,M] ).
Proof. intros * HA HS HM HB HMs; sem_intro; [ assumption | exact (rel_exp_under_ctx_let_zeta HS HA HM HMs HB) ]. Qed.
