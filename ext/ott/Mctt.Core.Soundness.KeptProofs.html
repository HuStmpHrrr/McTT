Mctt.Core.Soundness.KeptProofs

The Readback of a Kept Proof Is Typed

A proof is never evaluated: it is kept as the term it was written as, together with the environment it was met in (⋆ᵈ⟨N, ρ⟩), and reads back as N copied under local definitions binding the positions of ρ it reads (read_proof_kept). Nothing in the gluing model types that readback: the gluing is closed under the PERs, which relate a kept proof to any other proof, so no clause of it can speak of the value's own readback. For example, with Γ = (A : Type@0) (P : Prop) (f : P → A) (h : P), the real value of f h and the value f ⋆ᵈ⟨𝕃@0, nil⟩ are related by per_bot — their readbacks differ only under nf_proof, which nf_eq_upto_proofs ignores — and the readback of the second, f 𝕃@0, is not typed.
So the readback of a kept proof is typed under a hypothesis on its environment instead, stated here and proved for the environments the model builds: the definitions the readback of ρ produces are a well-formed context extension, and the substitution they ζ-reduce to is typed (env_lets_typed). The syntactic work is lets_exp_of_sub.

The definitions the readback of the first k positions of ρ produces number k: one per position.
Lemma read_lets_length : forall k ρ s bs, Rlets ρ at k in gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ⍮ s ↘ bs ->
    List.length bs = k.
Proof.
  intros k; induction k as [| k IH]; intros * H; inversion H; subst; cbn [List.length]; [ reflexivity |].
  f_equal; eapply IH; eassumption.
Qed.

ρ stands for the context Γ over Γ0: at every Kripke weakening of Γ0, the definitions the readback of ρ's first List.length Γ positions produces bind a well-formed extension whose first entries are those of Γ, and ζ-reduce to a substitution of Γ. The two conjuncts are the premises lets_exp_of_sub asks of a chain of definitions, at the substitution Id of the bound positions.
Definition env_lets_typed (Γ0 Γ : ctx) (ρ : env) : Prop :=
  forall Δ φ bs, Δ ⊢ᵏ φ : Γ0 ->
    Rlets ρ at (List.length Γ) in gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ⍮ (List.length Δ) ↘ bs ->
    gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ⍮ ctx_lets Δ bs ⊢ˢ Id : Γ /\
    gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ⍮ Δ ⊢ˢ lets_sub bs : Γ.

The readback of a kept proof whose environment stands for the context its term is typed in is typed, and equal to the term at the substitution the definitions ζ-reduce to (lets_exp_of_sub). This is what makes the normal form of a proof position typable, and so the normal form of a term typable where the gluing alone leaves it open.
Lemma kept_proof_readback_typed : forall Γ0 Γ ρ N P,
    env_lets_typed Γ0 Γ ρ ->
    List.length Γ = proof_scope N ρ ->
    Γ ⊢ N : P ->
    Γ ⊢ P : Propₘ ->
    forall Δ φ R, Δ ⊢ᵏ φ : Γ0 ->
      Rprf ⋆ᵈ⟨N, ρ⟩ in gc_deps ⍮ gc_stack ⍮ (List.length Δ) ↘ R ->
      exists σ, Δ ⊢ˢ σ : Γ /\ Δ ⊢ R : P[σ] /\ Δ ⊢ R ≈ N[σ] : P[σ].
Proof.
  intros * Henv Hlen HN HP * Hφ HR.
The readback of a kept proof is its term under the definitions of the positions it reads
  inversion HR; subst.
  match goal with Hb : Rlets ρ at proof_scope N ρ in _ ⍮ _ ⍮ _ ↘ _ |- _ => rename Hb into Hbs end.
  rewrite <- Hlen in Hbs.
  destruct (Henv _ _ _ Hφ Hbs) as [Hid Hsub].
  eexists; split; [ exact Hsub |].
The term is scoped by the definitions: they are one per position, and the term is scoped by the context it is typed in
  assert (Hsc : exp_scoped (List.length _) N)
    by (rewrite (read_lets_length _ _ _ _ Hbs); exact (proj1 (proj2 (proj1 (proj2 wf_scoped) _ _ _ _ _ HN)))).
  assert (Hz : Δ ⊢ˢ Id ⨟ lets_sub _ ≈ lets_sub _ : Γ)
    by (eapply wf_sub_eq_respects_sb_eq; [ apply wf_sub_eq_refl; exact Hsub | symmetry; apply sb_compose_id_left ]).
  destruct (lets_exp_of_sub _ HN HP Hsc Hid ltac:(intros; reflexivity) Hz) as [Ht Heq].
  split; [ exact Ht | exact Heq ].
Qed.
