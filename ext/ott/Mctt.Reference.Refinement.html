Mctt.Reference.Refinement

The Extracted Normalizers Refine the Reference Ones

The input–output equalities of the refinement of normalization: the normalizers of Extraction.NbE and those of Reference.NbE both compute the normal form nbe / nbe_ty specifies, which is unique (functional_nbe, functional_nbe_ty), so they give the same normal form. No simulation is needed here: the extracted normalizers are proved to meet the specification in Extraction.NbE, through the simulation of Extraction.Simulation. The checkers' agreement is Reference.TypeCheckAgreement.
The extracted code runs on a fast context (FCtx, Extraction.Lookup) and nothing else, so the equalities are stated at every fast context F, at the Core context it stands for (fc_deps F, fc_stack F). Those are the canonical Core contexts, which every well-formed one is (canon_of_wf). They are also stated at any canonical Θ ⍮ Ξ itself, with the reference side at Θ ⍮ Ξ and the extracted side at its translation fctx_of (the _at corollaries).

  Variables (F : FCtx) (Θ : gdeps) (Ξ : gstack).
  Hypotheses (HΘ : fc_deps F = Θ) (HΞ : fc_stack F = Ξ).

  Lemma nbe_impl_refines_eq : forall G M A H H',
      proj1_sig (Reference.NbE.nbe_impl Θ Ξ G M A H) = proj1_sig (Extraction.NbE.nbe_impl F G M A H').
  Proof.
    intros.
    destruct (Reference.NbE.nbe_impl Θ Ξ G M A H) as [W HW],
             (Extraction.NbE.nbe_impl F G M A H') as [W' HW']; cbn.
    rewrite HΘ, HΞ in HW'.
    eapply functional_nbe; eassumption.
  Qed.
