Semantic Weakenings

All three lemmas concern the bare rel_wk, the form the proofs use: the two context witnesses of rel_wk_under_ctx are carried by whatever produced them. Each holds because eval_wk is a function and the corresponding equation for ⟪_⟫ (eval_wk_id, eval_wk_shift, eval_wk_compose) holds by reflexivity, which is what fails for substitutions.
⟪wk_id⟫ ρ is ρ, so this is the identity.
Lemma rel_wk_id : forall R, rel_wk wk_id R R.
Proof.
  intros R; split; [ apply wk_mono_id |]; intros ρ ρ' H; rewrite !eval_wk_id; exact H.
Qed.

A weakening acts contravariantly on environments, so the composite φ ⊙ ψ — ψ after φ on variables — is φ after ψ here.
Lemma rel_wk_compose : forall {φ ψ R R' R''},
    rel_wk ψ R R' ->
    rel_wk φ R' R'' ->
    rel_wk (φ ⊙ ψ) R R''.
Proof.
  intros * [Hψm Hψ] [Hφm Hφ]; split; [ apply wk_mono_compose; assumption |].
  intros ρ ρ' H; rewrite !eval_wk_compose by assumption.
  exact (Hφ _ _ (Hψ _ _ H)).
Qed.

⟪↑⟫ ρ is ρ↯, and the Ctx-Ext biconditional says precisely that an environment of an extended context has a related tail. This holds for an assumption entry and a definition entry alike.
Lemma rel_wk_shift : forall {Γ e R R'},
    EF Γ ≈ Γ ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ R ->
    EF (e :: Γ)%list ≈ (e :: Γ)%list ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ R' ->
    rel_wk ↑ R' R.
Proof.
  intros * HΓ HΓA; split; [ apply wk_mono_shift |]; intros ρ ρ' H; rewrite !eval_wk_shift.
  inversion HΓA; subst; handle_per_ctx_env_irrel;
    apply_relation_equivalence; destruct_conjs; destruct_cons_rels; destruct_def_rels; destruct_dm_rels; destruct_mod_rels;
    eassumption.
Qed.

Evaluating the Identity and the Weakenings at a Context

The identity evaluates to the environment retagged at the context, and a weakening to its image retagged (ctx_retag): each entry is the corresponding entry of the environment, stored at the context's type and unit. A context PER relates the retagged environments as it relates the environments (per_ctx_env_retag).

Lemma eval_sub_id_rel : forall {Γ R ρ ρ'},

    ⟦ Id : Γ ⟧s ρ ↘ ctx_retag Γ ρ /\ ⟦ Id : Γ ⟧s ρ' ↘ ctx_retag Γ ρ'.
Proof.
  intros * HΓ Hρ; split; apply eval_sub_id.
Qed.

    (forall x, ctx_entry_type Δ1 x = ctx_entry_type Δ2 x) ->
    List.length Δ1 = List.length Δ2 ->
    (forall x, ctx_entry_unit Δ1 x = ctx_entry_unit Δ2 x) ->
    ⟦ σ : Δ1 ⟧s ρ ↘ ρσ ->
    ⟦ σ : Δ2 ⟧s ρ ↘ ρσ.

  assert (I : entry_propositional gc_deps gc_stack Δ2 ρσ x <-> entry_propositional gc_deps gc_stack Δ1 ρσ x).
  { split; intros [T HT E HE a Ea Hp]; econstructor; try eassumption;
      first [ rewrite Ht; eassumption | rewrite <- Ht; eassumption | rewrite Hsk; eassumption | rewrite <- Hsk; eassumption ]. }

Qed.

At an environment a context PER relates, an entry of a type whose values the PER's pairs relate is a proposition exactly when its value there is. The entry reads its type up to pointwise equality of environments, which the context PER respects.
Lemma head_propositional_iff : forall {Δ R e A i ρ ρ' a},
    EF Δ ≈ Δ ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ R ->
    ctx_entry_type (e :: nil) 0 = Some A ->
    (forall ρ ρ', Dom ρ ≈ ρ' ∈ R -> exists a a', ⟦ A ⟧ ρ ↘ a /\ ⟦ A ⟧ ρ' ↘ a' /\ Dom a ≈ a' ∈ per_univ i) ->
    Dom ρ ≈ ρ' ∈ R ->
    ⟦ A ⟧ ρ ↘ a ->
    head_propositional gc_deps gc_stack e Δ ρ <-> domain_kind a = ak_propositional.
Proof.
  intros * HΔ He HA Hρ Ha.
  assert (He' : ctx_entry_type (e :: Δ) 0 = Some A) by exact He.
  split.
  - intros [T HT E HE v Ev Hv]; rewrite He' in HT; injection HT as <-.
    cbn [List.skipn] in HE.
    assert (HE' : Dom E ≈ ρ' ∈ R)
      by (eapply per_ctx_env_resp_agree; [ exact HΔ | symmetry; exact HE | reflexivity | exact Hρ ]).
    destruct (HA _ _ HE') as (a1 & a1' & Ea1 & Ea1' & [R1 H1]).
    destruct (HA _ _ Hρ) as (a2 & a2' & Ea2 & Ea2' & [R2 H2]).
    functional_eval_rewrite_clear.
    pose proof (per_univ_elem_kind _ _ _ _ H1).
    pose proof (per_univ_elem_kind _ _ _ _ H2).
    congruence.
  - intros Hr; econstructor; [ exact He' | intros y _; reflexivity | exact Ha | exact Hr ].
Qed.

Instantiation at the Identity

A semantic judgment quantifies over semantic substitutions; instantiating it at Id gives a two-value statement. The Kripke quantification is instantiated at wk_id via rel_wk_id, and Id evaluates to the environment itself (eval_sub_id), which the universal form of rel_exp_under_ctx allows naming as the substituted environment. Since M[Id] is syntactically M (exp_sub_id), both commutation obligations of each chain compare a value with itself, and only the middle link remains.         Dom ρσ ≈ ρ'σ' ∈ env_relΔ.

Instances at Retagged Environments

A substitution of variables (Id, a weakening, Wk) evaluates to the environment it reads retagged at its target (eval_sub_id, ctx_retag): an instance of a judgment along it has its inner values at the retaggings. The Id instance at the environments themselves has the same inner values, and its outer values are at the environments, so the two chains merge there. These two lemmas read a judgment's body at a substitution whose results are retaggings, with the inner values at the environments retagged, for example ⟪φ⟫ ρ for ι φ at ρ.
Lemma rel_exp_body_of_retagged : forall {Γ T M M' RΓ} {i : nat},
    EF Γ ≈ Γ ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ RΓ ->
    (forall Γ' R' (_ : EF Γ' ≈ Γ' ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ R') σ σ',
        Γ' ⊨ˢ σ ≈ σ' : Γ ->
        forall ρ ρ' ρσ ρ'σ',
          Dom ρ ≈ ρ' ∈ R' -> ⟦ σ : Γ ⟧s ρ ↘ ρσ -> ⟦ σ' : Γ ⟧s ρ' ↘ ρ'σ' ->
          exists R, rel_typ i T σ ρ ρσ T σ' ρ' ρ'σ' R /\
            (type_propositional T σ ρ \/ rel_exp M σ ρ ρσ M' σ' ρ' ρ'σ' R)) ->
    forall Γ' R' (_ : EF Γ' ≈ Γ' ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ R') σ σ',
      Γ' ⊨ˢ σ ≈ σ' : Γ ->
      forall ρ ρ' ρσ ρ'σ',
        Dom ρ ≈ ρ' ∈ R' ->
        ⟦ σ : Γ ⟧s ρ ↘ ctx_retag Γ ρσ ->
        ⟦ σ' : Γ ⟧s ρ' ↘ ctx_retag Γ ρ'σ' ->
        Dom ρσ ≈ ρ'σ' ∈ RΓ ->
        exists R, rel_typ i T σ ρ ρσ T σ' ρ' ρ'σ' R /\
          (type_propositional T σ ρ \/ rel_exp M σ ρ ρσ M' σ' ρ' ρ'σ' R).
Proof.
  intros * HΓ HM * HΓ' * Hσ * Hρ Hev Hev' Hρσ.
  destruct (HM _ _ HΓ' _ _ Hσ _ _ _ _ Hρ Hev Hev') as [R1 [Ht1 He1]].
  destruct (eval_sub_id_rel HΓ Hρσ) as [Hid Hid'].
  destruct (HM _ _ HΓ _ _ (rel_sub_id (ex_intro _ _ HΓ)) _ _ _ _ Hρσ Hid Hid') as [R2 [Ht2 He2]].
  destruct Ht1 as [a1 a2 a3 a4 Ha1 Ha2 Ha3 Ha4 Hac].
  destruct Ht2 as [b1 b2 b3 b4 Hb1 Hb2 Hb3 Hb4 Hbc].
  rewrite exp_sub_id in Hb1, Hb4.
  assert (b2 = a2) as -> by (eapply functional_eval_exp; eassumption).
  assert (b3 = a3) as -> by (eapply functional_eval_exp; eassumption).

  assert (Hmid2 : DF a2 ≈ a3 ∈ per_univ_elem i ↘ R2) by pairwise.
  handle_per_univ_elem_irrel.
  assert (Hty : rel_chain (per_univ_elem i R1) ([a1; b1; b4; a4])) by merge_rel_chain Hac Hbc a2.
  exists R1; split; [ apply (mk_rel_exp a1 b1 b4 a4); assumption |].
  destruct He1 as [Hp | [v1 v2 v3 v4 Hv1 Hv2 Hv3 Hv4 Hc1]]; [ left; exact Hp |].
  destruct He2 as [(c & Hc & Hp) | [w1 w2 w3 w4 Hw1 Hw2 Hw3 Hw4 Hc2]].

    rewrite exp_sub_id in Hc.

Precomposition with a Weakening

Two instantiations of the same hypothesis at two weakenings. Given Γ' ⊨ʷ φ : Γ and ρ ≈ ρ' ∈ R_Γ', instantiate Δ ⊨ˢ σ ≈ σ' : Δ':
  • at Γ' with the composite ψ ⊙ φ (rel_wk_compose), whose outer values are the outer values wanted, because σ[ψ][φ] is σ[ψ ⊙ φ] (sb_wk_wk);
  • at Δ with ψ and the weakened pair ⟪φ⟫ ρ ≈ ⟪φ⟫ ρ', whose outer values are the inner values wanted.

Changing the Context PER

A substitution judgment may be read with a smaller domain relation or a larger codomain relation, and a term judgment in any context whose relation is included in that of its own context. This is how a judgment about Γ ▹ A serves for Γ ▸ A ≔ M, and a judgment about Γ ▸ A ≔ M for Γ ▸ A ≔ M' when M and M' are related. As evaluation is directed by the target, the larger codomain and the smaller one are to agree on which of the smaller one's entries are propositional (ctx_props_agree): each is, in practice, a context of the same entry types, or a prefix of it.
The entries of Δ2, at the environments S relates, are propositional entries of Δ1 exactly when they are of Δ2: an evaluation at Δ1 holds a kept proof at the one and a value at the other, as an evaluation at Δ2 asks.
Definition ctx_props_agree (S : relation env) (Δ1 Δ2 : ctx) : Prop :=
  forall ρ ρ', Dom ρ ≈ ρ' ∈ S -> forall x, x < List.length Δ2 ->
    entry_propositional gc_deps gc_stack Δ1 ρ x <-> entry_propositional gc_deps gc_stack Δ2 ρ x.

Lemma ctx_props_agree_same_types : forall S Δ1 Δ2,

Qed.
