The ι Rule of a Group of Inductive Types

The semantic rule of ι: the eliminator at a constructor of a member is the constructor's branch at the arguments and the recursive results. It holds because the group is valid at the target of a sound embedding (valid_groups_of_embedding), where the two sides are related (Inductives/Iota.v).
