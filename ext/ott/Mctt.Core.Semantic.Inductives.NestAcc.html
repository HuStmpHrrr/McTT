Mctt.Core.Semantic.Inductives.NestAcc

The Nesting Order on Semantic Types Is Well Founded

The view search of a group declaration (views_dfs, in Core.Syntactic.Inductives.Group) starts from the nested occurrences written in the declaration and follows, from each instance it meets, the instances nested in the argument types of its constructors. Its termination is read off the PER model.
grp_step b a: a is the type value of a declared member of a filed group at the uniform parameters ups, and b is related to the shape end (the end of a chain of Πs, read at fresh variables) of a type related to the value of a non-recursive argument type of a constructor of *any* member of that group (declared or view), at ups and at elements of the member's non-uniform parameters and of the arguments before (tele_elems). These are the edges of the view search that leave a group's cluster (the members of the group at ups). The non-uniform parameters ns of a, and which declared member a is, play no part. Likewise out of a record type at its parameters (grp_step_record): b is related to the shape end of a type related to a field's type, at the parameters and elements of the fields before; these are the edges out of a record view, its own cluster.
Theorem per_univ_nest_acc: every type of the PER model, every type related to one, and every shape end of those, is accessible for grp_step (nest_acc). The proof is the induction principle of the PER model (per_univ_elem_ind): the clause for inductive types carries, for every member and every constructor, the derivation of each non-recursive argument's type at every related environment, with its induction hypothesis; the clause for record types carries the same for each field's type (fields_evals). Accessibility is invariant along the relation (nest_acc_rel).

One step of nesting, out of a group at ups, or out of a record type at its parameters.
  Inductive grp_step : domain -> domain -> Prop :=
  
A step out of a member of a group at ups: into a non-recursive argument's type
  | grp_step_intro : forall b p k D ups ns m Mm c gs j A ms vs b0 i0 R0 z e i2 R2,
      
a is a member of the group D at p at ups
      ind_lookup gc_deps gc_stack p = Some D ->
      List.length ups = ind_nu D ->
      
the j-th non-recursive argument A of the constructor c of the member m
      ind_member D m = Some Mm ->
      ind_con Mm c = Some gs ->
      List.nth_error (cargs_nonrec gs) j = Some A ->
      
at elements of the member's non-uniform parameters and of the arguments before
      tele_elems (decl_env D ups) (List.rev (im_nparams Mm)) ms ->
      List.length ms = List.length (im_nparams Mm) ->
      tele_elems (components_env D ups (List.rev (im_nparams Mm)) ms) (cargs_nonrec_tele gs) vs ->
      List.length vs = j ->
      ⟦ A ⟧ components_env D ups (List.rev (im_nparams Mm) ++ cargs_nonrec_tele gs) (ms ++ vs) ↘ b0 ->
      
b is related to a shape end of a type related to it
      DF z ≈ b0 ∈ per_univ_elem i0 ↘ R0 ->
      shape_end e z ->
      DF b ≈ e ∈ per_univ_elem i2 ↘ R2 ->
      grp_step b (d_ind p k (ups ++ ns))
  
A step out of a record type at ns: into a field's type
  | grp_step_record : forall b p R ns j f T vs b0 i0 R0 z e i2 R2,
      
a is the record type at p at ns
      record_lookup gc_deps gc_stack p = Some R ->
      
the j-th field f, of type T
      List.nth_error (record_fields R) j = Some (f, T) ->
      
at elements of the fields before
      tele_elems (env_tele nil (List.rev (record_params R)) ns) (record_fields_tele R) vs ->
      List.length vs = j ->
      ⟦ T ⟧ record_fields_env R ns vs ↘ b0 ->
      
b is related to a shape end of a type related to it
      DF z ≈ b0 ∈ per_univ_elem i0 ↘ R0 ->
      shape_end e z ->
      DF b ≈ e ∈ per_univ_elem i2 ↘ R2 ->
      grp_step b (d_record p ns).

  Lemma tele_elems_transfer : forall (P : relation domain -> domain -> domain -> Prop)
      (HP : forall R x y, P R x y -> exists i : universe_index, DF x ≈ y ∈ per_univ_elem i ↘ R) tr ρ1 ρ2 T vs,

      (forall k κ A vs1 vs2, List.nth_error T k = Some (ce_ass κ A) -> tele_prefix_rel tr k vs1 vs2 ->
         rel_mod_eval P A (env_tele ρ1 T vs1) A (env_tele ρ2 T vs2) (tr k vs1)) ->

    intros * HP tr ρ1 ρ2 T vs H Hev; induction H as [| vs κ A t i R v H IH HA Ht Htt Hv].

      pose proof (functional_eval_exp _ _ _ _ Ht E1) as <-.
      destruct (HP _ _ _ HPt) as [i' Ht12].
