The View Equations of a Filed Group, Read in the PER Model

The semantic view equations of a group (views_self_valid) are judgments over the context of its members as variables (grp_selfs_ctx). Once the group is filed at p, the substitution grp_sb p D of its type formers at the uniform parameters for those variables is built from valid parts (grp_sb_gsub), and moves each equation to the filed group: a view's instance is typed at the uniform parameters, and its non-uniform parameters and the arguments of its constructors are its container's at the instance. These are the semantic counterparts of the syntactic decl_typed_view_* lemmas of Core.Syntactic.System.Global.Presup.

      per_tele_args (List.rev Δ) nil nil ys xs.
  Proof.

    destruct (rel_ctx_wf_per_ctx_env HW) as [RU HRU].
