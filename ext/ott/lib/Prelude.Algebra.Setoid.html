Prelude›Algebra›Setoid

Setoids: IsEquivalence A eq holds when eq is reflexive, symmetric and transitive, and the bundle Setoid is a carrier with such a relation. Every structure of Prelude›Algebra states its laws up to a relation eq, so that a quotient-like carrier, as the integers as differences of numbers in Prelude›Algebra›Integers, is an instance, and so is the Leibniz equality of Prelude›Logic›Leibniz, through Prelude›Algebra›Leibniz.

Each unit of the hierarchy takes one level l, of both the carrier, A : Type@{l}, and the relation, eq : A → A → Type@{l}. A carrier and a relation at different levels are both at the larger one, by cumulativity. The equality of numbers Eq and the relation Same of Prelude›Algebra›Integers are at the level of their carrier, 0l; the Leibniz equality of a type of Type@{u} lives one level up, so a Leibniz instance is at succl u, where the carrier is too. A predicate lives in Type@{l} and a bundle in Type@{succl l}.

module Prelude›Algebra›Setoid (l : Level) where

record IsEquivalence (A : Type@{l}) (eq : ∀ (x : A) (y : A) → Type@{l}) :
    Type@{l} where
  refl : ∀ (x : A) → eq x x;
  sym : ∀ (x : A) (y : A) (h : eq x y) → eq y x;
  trans : ∀ (x : A) (y : A) (z : A) (hxy : eq x y) (hyz : eq y z) → eq x z
end

record Setoid : Type@{succl l} where
  carrier : Type@{l};
  eq : ∀ (x : carrier) (y : carrier) → Type@{l};
  isEquivalence : IsEquivalence carrier eq
with
  open isEquivalence export (refl; sym; trans)
end

end
