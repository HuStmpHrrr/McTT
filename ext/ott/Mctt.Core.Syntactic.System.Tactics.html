Mctt.Core.Syntactic.System.Tactics

Saturating the Context with Presuppositions

The rules mention only the premises they need, but almost every proof about them needs more: that the context is well-formed, that the type of a term is a type, that a looked-up binding is a type. All of these are consequences of the premises, and the tactics here add them to the context once, so that the rules can afterwards be applied by mauto without the search having to rediscover them at every step.
Each tactic is idempotent: a presupposition that is already present — even marked by on_all_hyp: — is not added a second time. That is what makes them safe to compose and to call repeatedly.

From Mctt Require Import LibTactics.
From Mctt.Core Require Import Base.
From Mctt.Core.Syntactic.System Require Export Definitions Lemmas.
From Mctt.Core.Syntactic.System.Inductives Require Export Typing Views UniformParams.
Import Syntax_Notations Wk_Notations.

The type A of a typed term HM : Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A, its type being HAi : Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ᵗ A, is added as its sort, the sort's type_sort and the typing, unless the context already has A at a universe, at Propₘ, or at a sort it knows to be one. A is of a sort when M is no former of a type of levels (presup_exp_typ_not_level); otherwise A may be the sort LevelSort itself, of no sort, and HAi is kept as it is.
Ltac add_type Θ Ξ Γ M A HM HAi :=
  match goal with
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@_ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@_ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Propₘ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : Propₘ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : ?S, _: type_sort ?S |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : ?S, _: type_sort ?S |- _ => clear HAi
  | _ => lazymatch A with LevelSort => clear HAi | _ => fail end
  | _ =>
      let Hn := fresh "Hn" in
      assert (Hn : ~ level_type_former M) by (inversion 1);
      let S := fresh "S" in
      let HS := fresh "HS" in
      let HA := fresh "HA" in
      destruct (presup_exp_typ_not_level HM Hn) as (S & HS & HA); clear Hn HAi
  
or when A is visibly another type than LevelSort
  | _ =>
      let Hn := fresh "Hn" in
      assert (Hn : A <> LevelSort) by discriminate; clear Hn;
      let Hc := fresh "Hc" in
      let S := fresh "S" in
      let HS := fresh "HS" in
      let HA := fresh "HA" in
      pose proof (presup_exp_typ_cases HM) as Hc;
      inversion Hc as [? S HS HA | ? ?]; clear Hc HAi
  | _ => idtac
  end.

A type known to be of a sort, by the tactic tac that adds its sort, the sort's type_sort and the typing, unless the context already has A at a sort; HAi : ⊢ᵗ A is dropped either way.
Ltac add_sorted_type Θ Ξ Γ A HAi tac :=
  match goal with
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@_ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@_ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Propₘ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : Propₘ |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : ?S, _: type_sort ?S |- _ => clear HAi
  | _: Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : ?S, _: type_sort ?S |- _ => clear HAi
  | _ => clear HAi; tac
  end.

ctx_decomp on a single context well-formedness hypothesis. The type of the top binding is only added if the context does not already have it.
#[local]
Ltac invert_wf_ctx1 H :=
  match type of H with
  | ⊢ ?Θ ⍮ ?Ξ ⍮ ?Γ ▹{_} ?A =>
      let HΓ := fresh "HΓ" in
      let HAi := fresh "HAi" in
      pose proof ctx_decomp H as [HΓ HAi];
      
the entry is at a sort of its kind, never at LevelSort
      add_sorted_type Θ Ξ Γ A HAi ltac:(
        let S := fresh "S" in let HS := fresh "HS" in let HA := fresh "HA" in let Hk := fresh "Hk" in
        destruct (proj2 (ctx_decomp_sort_kind H)) as (S & Hk & HA); pose proof (sort_kind_type_sort _ _ Hk) as HS; clear Hk)
  | ⊢ ?Θ ⍮ ?Ξ ⍮ ?Γ ▸ ?A ≔ ?M =>
      let HΓ := fresh "HΓ" in
      let HAi := fresh "HAi" in
      let HM := fresh "HM" in
      pose proof ctx_decomp_def H as [HΓ [HAi HM]];
      add_sorted_type Θ Ξ Γ A HAi ltac:(
        let S := fresh "S" in let HS := fresh "HS" in let HA := fresh "HA" in
        destruct (ctx_decomp_def_sorted H) as (S & HS & HA))
  end.

Ltac invert_wf_ctx :=
  (on_all_hyp: fun H => invert_wf_ctx1 H);
  clear_dups.

The presuppositions that need no induction over equality: presup_exp_typ for typing, and the two projections of a weakening or a substitution. The wf_wk and wf_sub cases are exactly saturate_wk and saturate_sub, which gen_core_presups calls.
Ltac gen_core_presup H :=
  match type of H with
  | ?Θ ⍮ ?Ξ ⍮ ?Γ ⊢ ?M : ?A =>
      let HΓ := fresh "HΓ" in
      let HAi := fresh "HAi" in
      pose proof presup_exp H as [HΓ HAi];
      match goal with
      | _: __mark__ _ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@_ |- _ => clear HAi
      | _: __mark__ _ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Propₘ |- _ => clear HAi
      | _: __mark__ _ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : ?S, _: type_sort ?S |- _ => clear HAi
      | _ => add_type Θ Ξ Γ M A H HAi
      end
  end.

Ltac gen_lookup_presup H :=
  match type of H with
  | ?Γ ∋ #?x : ?A =>
      match goal with
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@_ |- _ => fail
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@_ |- _ => fail
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : Propₘ |- _ => fail
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : Propₘ |- _ => fail
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : ?S, _: type_sort ?S |- _ => fail
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ A : ?S, _: type_sort ?S |- _ => fail
      | _ =>
          let S := fresh "S" in
          let HS := fresh "HS" in
          let HA := fresh "HA" in
          pose proof (ctx_lookup_wf_sorted _ _ _ _ _ ltac:(eassumption) H) as (S & HS & HA)
      end
  | ?Γ ∋ #?x ≔ ?M : ?A =>
      match goal with
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A |- _ => fail
      | _: _ ⍮ _ ⍮ __mark__ _ Γ ⊢ M : A |- _ => fail
      | _ =>
          let HM := fresh "HM" in
          pose proof (ctx_lookup_def_wf _ _ _ _ _ _ ltac:(eassumption) H) as HM
      end
  end.

Ltac gen_core_presups :=
  (on_all_hyp: fun H => gen_core_presup H);
  invert_wf_ctx;
  (on_all_hyp: fun H => gen_lookup_presup H);
  saturate_wk;
  saturate_sub;
  clear_dups.
