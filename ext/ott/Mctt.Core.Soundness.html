Soundness of Normalization by Evaluation

The fundamental theorem of the gluing model, instantiated at the identity embedding and the identity substitution.
During milestone M1 of the Prop landing the gluing model (Core/Soundness/*) is not ported to relevance-directed evaluation: the files are out of the build, and the theorems below keep their statements with their proofs pending (PENDING M2). The proofs before the landing are in the history of this file.

The readback of a level of sort n is a canonical level of sort n: its constant is of a tier at most n, and each atom is a level of its own sort (level_atoms_well_scoped). The atoms' sorts are read off the types of the neutrals, and this is what makes them reliable: the equations of a canonical form drop an atom by absorption, which needs it typed at its sort.
Definition nf_lvl_well_scoped (n : nat) (Δ : ctx) (L : nf) : Prop :=
  match L with
  | nf_lvl c xs => fst c <= n /\ @level_atoms_well_scoped gc_deps gc_stack n Δ xs
  | _ => False
  end.

End Fixed_GCtx.

A typed term has a normal form, equal to it: it glues by the fundamental theorem of the gluing model, read at the identity embedding.
Theorem soundness : forall {Θ Ξ Γ M A},
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A ->
    exists W, nbe Θ Ξ Γ M A W /\ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M ≈ W : A.
Proof.
  (* PENDING M2: the gluing model for the Prop core *)
Admitted.

Two terms of one type whose normal forms are equal up to their proofs (nf_eq_upto_proofs) are equal: completeness compares normal forms only up to proofs, and proofs are equal by proof irrelevance wherever they occur. For example, with f : P → ℕ and h1 h2 : P, the normal forms of f h1 and f h2 are ⇑ⁿ{ak_relevant} (f $ⁿ nf_proof #1) and ⇑ⁿ{ak_relevant} (f $ⁿ nf_proof #0), related, and f h1 ≈ f h2 : ℕ holds by proof irrelevance under the application. Milestone M2 proves it from the gluing model.
Theorem soundness_upto_proofs : forall {Θ Ξ Γ M M' A W W'},
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A ->
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M' : A ->
    nbe Θ Ξ Γ M A W ->
    nbe Θ Ξ Γ M' A W' ->
    nf_eq_upto_proofs W W' ->
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M ≈ M' : A.
Proof.
  (* PENDING M2: the gluing model for the Prop core *)
Admitted.

The normal form NbE computes is equal to the term.
Theorem soundness' : forall {Θ Ξ Γ M A W},
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A ->
    nbe Θ Ξ Γ M A W ->
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M ≈ W : A.
Proof.
  intros * [? []]%soundness ?.
  functional_nbe_rewrite_clear.
  eassumption.
Qed.

Lemma soundness_ty : forall {Γ} {i : nat} {A},
    Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@i ->
    exists W, nbe_ty_f Γ A W /\ Γ ⊢ A ≈ W : Typeω@i.
Proof.
  intros.
  assert (exists W', nbe_f Γ A Typeω@i W' /\ Γ ⊢ A ≈ W' : Typeω@i) as [? [?%nbe_type_to_nbe_ty Heq]] by mauto using soundness.
  firstorder.
Qed.

Lemma soundness_ty' : forall {Γ} {i : nat} {A B},
    Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@i ->
    nbe_ty_f Γ A B ->
    Γ ⊢ A ≈ B : Typeω@i.
Proof.
  intros.
  assert (exists B', nbe_ty_f Γ A B' /\ Γ ⊢ A ≈ B' : Typeω@i) as [? [? Heq]] by mauto using soundness_ty.
  functional_nbe_rewrite_clear.
  eassumption.
Qed.

A type of a sort (type_sort) is equal there to its normal form.
Lemma soundness_ty_at_sort : forall {Γ S A W},
    type_sort S ->
    Γ ⊢ A : S ->
    nbe_ty_f Γ A W ->
    Γ ⊢ A ≈ W : S.
Proof.
  intros * [i | |] HA Hn;
    [ exact (soundness_ty' HA Hn)
    | exact (soundness' HA (nbe_ty_to_nbe_prop _ _ _ Hn))
    | exact (soundness' HA (nbe_ty_to_nbe_level_sort _ _ _ Hn)) ].
Qed.

The normal form of a level of sort n is a canonical level of sort n: its constant is of a tier at most n, and each atom is a level of its own sort (nf_lvl_well_scoped). It is read off the gluing of the level, whose atoms are glued at their types. The algorithmic layer needs it to trust the sorts an atom of a normal form carries.
Theorem soundness_lvl_well_scoped : forall {Γ M n W},
    Γ ⊢ M : Level@n ->
    nbe_f Γ M Level@n W ->
    nf_lvl_well_scoped n Γ W.

A neutral is an atom of sort s of the normal form of a typed level: the atoms whose coherence soundness gives (soundness_nbe_atoms_upto_proofs). The atom is one of the list (List.In), not a neutral equal to one up to proofs.

The atoms of a level normal form without proofs hold no proofs.
Lemma level_atoms_proof_free_In : forall c xs j s a,
    nf_proof_free (nf_lvl c xs) -> List.In (mk_level_atom j s a) xs -> ne_proof_free a.
Proof.
  intros c xs; cbn [nf_proof_free]; induction xs as [| x xs IH]; intros * Hp Hin; [ destruct Hin |].
  destruct Hp as [Hx Hxs]; destruct Hin as [-> | Hin]; [ exact Hx | exact (IH _ _ _ Hxs Hin) ].
Qed.

The atoms of a level of sort n read off its well scoped normal form (level_atoms_well_scoped) are levels of their sorts.
Lemma level_atoms_well_scoped_In : forall n Γ xs j s a,
    @level_atoms_well_scoped gc_deps gc_stack n Γ xs -> List.In (mk_level_atom j s a) xs -> Γ ⊢ (a : exp) : Level@s.
Proof.
  intros n Γ xs; induction xs as [| [k t b] xs IH]; intros * Hw Hin; [ destruct Hin |].
  destruct Hw as [[_ Hb] Hxs]; destruct Hin as [Heq | Hin];
    [ injection Heq as -> -> ->; exact Hb | exact (IH _ _ _ Hxs Hin) ].
Qed.

Two atoms of normal forms of levels, of one sort and equal up to their proofs, are equal levels: a canonical form keeps one of them in place of the other (level_atoms_coherent). Typed levels hold no proofs (nbe_level_proof_free), so the two atoms are the same neutral, a level of its sort (soundness_lvl_well_scoped). For example, the atom g (λ x : F h. x) with a proof h in an annotation cannot occur: F : P → LevelSort is not typable, LevelSort being in no universe.
Theorem soundness_nbe_atoms_upto_proofs : forall {Γ s a b},
    nbe_level_atom Γ s a ->
    nbe_level_atom Γ s b ->
    ne_eq_upto_proofs a b ->
    Γ ⊢ (a : exp) ≈ (b : exp) : Level@s.
Proof.
  intros * [Ma na ca xsa ja Hta Hnbea Hina] [Mb nb cb xsb jb Htb Hnbeb Hinb] Hab.
  assert (HΓ : ⊢ Γ) by (gen_presups; assumption).
The atom holds no proof: it is the other one
  pose proof (nbe_level_proof_free Hta (wf_level _ _ _ _ HΓ) Hnbea) as Hpf.
  pose proof (ne_proof_free_eq _ _ (level_atoms_proof_free_In _ _ _ _ _ Hpf Hina) Hab) as <-.
a level of its sort
  destruct (soundness_lvl_well_scoped Hta Hnbea) as [_ Hws].
  exact (wf_exp_eq_refl (level_atoms_well_scoped_In _ _ _ _ _ _ Hws Hina)).
Qed.

The atoms of the normal forms of typed levels are coherent.
Lemma level_atoms_coherent_of_nbe : forall {Γ} xs,
    (forall j s a, List.In (mk_level_atom j s a) xs -> nbe_level_atom Γ s a) ->
    @level_atoms_coherent gc_deps gc_stack Γ xs.
Proof.
  intros * Hxs j s a j' b Ha Hb Hab.
  exact (soundness_nbe_atoms_upto_proofs (Hxs _ _ _ Ha) (Hxs _ _ _ Hb) Hab).
Qed.

The atoms of the normal form of a typed level are atoms of normal forms of typed levels.
Lemma nbe_level_atom_of_nbe : forall {Γ M n c xs},

    forall j s a, List.In (mk_level_atom j s a) xs -> nbe_level_atom Γ s a.
Proof. intros * HM Hnbe j s a Hin; exact (mk_nbe_level_atom _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ HM Hnbe Hin). Qed.
