Mctt.Core.Soundness

Soundness of Normalization by Evaluation

The fundamental theorem of the gluing model, instantiated at the identity embedding and the identity substitution. The gluing model equates a term with its readback, whose proofs are tagged with their propositions (nf_to_exp): the term is equal to its normal form (soundness_of_glu). Two terms whose normal forms are equal up to proofs are then equal (soundness_upto_proofs).

A typed term that glues has a normal form equal to it: its glued value at the initial environment is read back (realize_glu_elem_top); it is the term's value at an evaluated kind and the term kept at a proposition, as nbe_term has it.
Lemma soundness_of_glu : forall {Γ M A},
    Γ ⊢ M : A ->
    Γ ⊩ M : A ->
    exists W, nbe_f Γ M A W /\ Γ ⊢ M ≈ W : A.
Proof.
  intros * H HG.
  assert (⊢ Γ) by mauto 3.
  assert (exists env_relΓ, EF Γ ≈ Γ ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ env_relΓ) as [env_relΓ]
      by mauto 3 using completeness_fundamental_ctx, rel_ctx_wf_per_ctx_env.
  destruct HG as [Sb ? i ?].
  pose proof (wf_ctx_scoped_exp H) as HsΓ.
  pose proof (per_ctx_then_per_env_initial_env ltac:(eassumption) HsΓ HsΓ) as [ρ [? ?]].
  destruct_conjs.
  functional_initial_env_rewrite_clear.
  assert (Γ ⊢ˢ Id ® ρ ∈ Sb) by (eapply initial_env_glu_rel_exp; mauto 3).
  destruct_glu_rel_exp_with_sub.
  pose proof (realize_glu_elem_top _ _ _ _ glu_rel_exp_with_sub_univ _ _ _ _ glu_rel_exp_with_sub_exp) as Htop.
  destruct Htop.
  destruct (glu_elem_top_per (length Γ)) as [W W0 HW _ _].
  assert (Hid : Γ ⊢ᵏ wk_id : Γ) by mauto 3.
  pose proof (glu_elem_top_read _ _ _ Hid HW) as HeqW; unfold glu_nf_eq in HeqW.
  rewrite !exp_wk_id, !exp_sub_id in HeqW.
  exists W; split; [| exact HeqW ].
  econstructor; [ eassumption | eassumption |].
The term's value at an evaluated kind, the term kept at a proposition
  destruct (domain_kind a) eqn:Hk; cbn [glu_term_value] in glu_rel_exp_with_sub_eval.
  - eapply nbe_term_evaluated; [ rewrite Hk; constructor | exact glu_rel_exp_with_sub_eval | exact HW ].
  - subst m; eapply nbe_term_proof; [ exact Hk | exact HW ].
  - eapply nbe_term_evaluated; [ rewrite Hk; constructor | exact glu_rel_exp_with_sub_eval | exact HW ].
Qed.

A typed term has a normal form, equal to it: it glues by the fundamental theorem of the gluing model, read at the identity embedding.
Theorem soundness : forall {Θ Ξ Γ M A},
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A ->
    exists W, nbe Θ Ξ Γ M A W /\ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M ≈ W : A.
Proof.
  intros * H.
  pose proof (@soundness_fundamental_exp (gc_mk Θ Ξ) _ _ _ H) as HG.
  exact (@soundness_of_glu (gc_mk Θ Ξ) _ _ _ H HG).
Qed.

Two terms of one type whose normal forms are equal up to their proofs (nf_eq_upto_proofs) are equal: completeness compares normal forms only up to proofs, and proofs are equal by proof irrelevance wherever they occur. For example, with f : P → ℕ and h1 h2 : P, the normal forms of f h1 and f h2 are ⇑ⁿ{ak_relevant} (f $ⁿ nf_proof #1) and ⇑ⁿ{ak_relevant} (f $ⁿ nf_proof #0), related, and f h1 ≈ f h2 : ℕ holds by proof irrelevance under the application. A corollary of exact soundness (soundness_of_glu): each term is equal to its normal form, and the two normal forms are equal (nf_to_exp_upto_proofs).
Theorem soundness_upto_proofs : forall {Θ Ξ Γ M M' A W W'},
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M : A ->
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M' : A ->
    nbe Θ Ξ Γ M A W ->
    nbe Θ Ξ Γ M' A W' ->
    nf_eq_upto_proofs W W' ->
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M ≈ M' : A.
Proof.
  intros * HM HM' Hn Hn' E.
  destruct (@soundness_of_glu (gc_mk Θ Ξ) _ _ _ HM (@soundness_fundamental_exp (gc_mk Θ Ξ) _ _ _ HM))
    as (W1 & Hn1 & HE1).
  destruct (@soundness_of_glu (gc_mk Θ Ξ) _ _ _ HM' (@soundness_fundamental_exp (gc_mk Θ Ξ) _ _ _ HM'))
    as (W2 & Hn2 & HE2).
  pose proof (functional_nbe _ _ _ _ _ Hn Hn1) as <-.
  pose proof (functional_nbe _ _ _ _ _ Hn' Hn2) as <-.
The two normal forms, typed, are equal up to their proofs: equal (nf_to_exp_upto_proofs)
  pose proof (nf_to_exp_upto_proofs _ _ _ _ _ _ (presup_exp_eq_right HE1) (ex_intro _ _ (presup_exp_eq_right HE2)) E) as H12.
  etransitivity; [ exact HE1 | etransitivity; [ exact H12 | symmetry; exact HE2 ] ].
Qed.

The normal form NbE computes is equal to the term.

    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ M ≈ W : A.

A type of a sort (type_sort) is equal there to its normal form (soundness').
Lemma soundness_ty_at_sort : forall {Γ S A W},
    type_sort S ->
    Γ ⊢ A : S ->
    nbe_ty_f Γ A W ->
    Γ ⊢ A ≈ W : S.
Proof. intros * HS HA Hn; exact (soundness' HA (nbe_ty_to_nbe_at_sort _ _ _ _ HS Hn)). Qed.

Lemma soundness_ty' : forall {Γ} {i : nat} {A B},
    Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@i ->
    nbe_ty_f Γ A B ->
    Γ ⊢ A ≈ B : Typeω@i.
Proof. intros * HA Hn; exact (soundness_ty_at_sort (type_sort_universe i) HA Hn). Qed.

Lemma soundness_ty : forall {Γ} {i : nat} {A},
    Γ ⊢ A : Typeω@i ->
    exists W, nbe_ty_f Γ A W /\ Γ ⊢ A ≈ W : Typeω@i.
Proof.
  intros * HA.
  destruct (soundness HA) as (W & Hn & HE).
  exists W; split; [ apply nbe_type_to_nbe_ty in Hn; exact Hn | exact HE ].
Qed.

Two types of a sort whose normal forms are equal up to their proofs are equal (soundness_upto_proofs).
Lemma soundness_ty_upto_proofs : forall {Γ S A B W W'},
    type_sort S ->
    Γ ⊢ A : S ->
    Γ ⊢ B : S ->
    nbe_ty_f Γ A W ->
    nbe_ty_f Γ B W' ->
    nf_eq_upto_proofs W W' ->
    Γ ⊢ A ≈ B : S.
Proof.
  intros * HS HA HB HnA HnB E.
  exact (soundness_upto_proofs HA HB (nbe_ty_to_nbe_at_sort _ _ _ _ HS HnA) (nbe_ty_to_nbe_at_sort _ _ _ _ HS HnB) E).
Qed.

The normal form of a level of sort n is a canonical level of sort n: its constant is of a tier at most n, and each atom is a level of its own sort (nf_lvl_well_scoped). It is read off the gluing of the level, whose atoms are glued at their types. The algorithmic layer needs it to trust the sorts an atom of a normal form carries.
Theorem soundness_lvl_well_scoped : forall {Γ M n W},
    Γ ⊢ M : Level@n ->
    nbe_f Γ M Level@n W ->
    nf_lvl_well_scoped n Γ W.
Proof.
  intros * H Hn.
  assert (⊢ Γ) by mauto 3.
  assert (exists env_relΓ, EF Γ ≈ Γ ∈ per_ctx_env ↘ env_relΓ) as [env_relΓ]
      by mauto 3 using completeness_fundamental_ctx, rel_ctx_wf_per_ctx_env.
  pose proof (soundness_fundamental_exp _ _ _ H) as HM.
  destruct HM as [Sb HSb _ _].
  pose proof (wf_ctx_scoped_exp H) as HsΓ.
  pose proof (per_ctx_then_per_env_initial_env ltac:(eassumption) HsΓ HsΓ) as [ρ [? ?]].
  destruct_conjs.
  functional_initial_env_rewrite_clear.
  assert (HId : Γ ⊢ˢ Id ® ρ ∈ Sb) by (eapply initial_env_glu_rel_exp; mauto 3).
  destruct (glu_rel_exp_level_elim (n := n) HSb (soundness_fundamental_exp _ _ _ H) _ _ _ HId)
    as [m [Hev [_ Hglu]]].
  destruct (nbe_evaluated_inv Hn ρ _ ltac:(eassumption) ltac:(econstructor) ltac:(cbn; constructor)) as (m' & Hm' & Hr).
  functional_eval_rewrite_clear.
  assert (Γ ⊢ᵏ wk_id : Γ) by mauto 3.
  exact (proj2 (Hglu _ _ _ ltac:(eassumption) (read_nf_level_sort _ _ 0 _ _ Hr))).
Qed.

Two atoms of normal forms of levels, of one sort and equal up to their proofs, are equal levels: a canonical form keeps one of them in place of the other (level_atoms_coherent). Typed levels hold no proofs (nbe_level_proof_free), so the two atoms are the same neutral, a level of its sort (soundness_lvl_well_scoped). For example, the atom g (λ x : F h. x) with a proof h in an annotation cannot occur: F : P → LevelSort is not typable, LevelSort being in no universe.
Theorem soundness_nbe_atoms_upto_proofs : forall {Γ s a b},
    nbe_level_atom Γ s a ->
    nbe_level_atom Γ s b ->
    ne_eq_upto_proofs a b ->
    Γ ⊢ (a : exp) ≈ (b : exp) : Level@s.
Proof.

  assert (HΓ : ⊢ Γ) by (gen_presups; assumption).

The atoms of the normal forms of typed levels are coherent.

    (forall j s a, List.In (mk_level_atom j s a) xs -> nbe_level_atom Γ s a) ->
    @level_atoms_coherent gc_deps gc_stack Γ xs.

Qed.
