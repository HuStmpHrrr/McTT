Mctt.Extraction.FastRead

The Readback the Extracted Checker Runs

A copy of the readback relations (Core.Semantic.Readback) over the evaluation of Extraction.FastEval: every closure is evaluated by fast_eval_exp and its twins. With the skip on (SkipMode), three rules differ:
  • the fresh recursive results of a branch of a stuck eliminator are computed only for an argument whose results the branch reads (ihs_skip_at); for one it reads none of, each variable is at 𝕌ω@0 (fast_fresh_ihs_skip), as fast_eval_ihs_skip stores 𝕌ω@0 for each result;
  • a kept proof reads back as its term, with no definitions around it (fast_read_proof_kept_skipping);
  • a type former at no argument reads back with no lookup (fast_read_typ_indty_nil).
With it off, the relations are those of Core.Semantic.Readback (Extraction.FastEquiv). Extraction.Simulation relates the two readbacks, up to proofs.
