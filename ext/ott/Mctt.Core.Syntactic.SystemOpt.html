The Rules with the Redundant Premises Removed

Every rule carries the premises a presupposition-free formulation needs: the Π-rules check the domain and the codomain, the ⊥-eliminator checks its type, the congruence rules check the left-hand side. A premise that tells a universe from Prop stays: the codomain of a λ at a Π is premised to be of a universe. Once presupposition is available all of those are consequences of the remaining premises, and this file restates each rule without them. The primed version is registered as a hint and the rule itself is unregistered, so mauto searches the small statement and never rediscovers a premise it could have presupposed.
gen_presups does all the work; impl_opt_constructor is the whole proof of most of them. Where more is needed it is always the same two things: a level at which the domain and the codomain of a Π-type are both types (the max of their two levels, or wf_pi_inversion' when the Π-type is already known to be one), and cumulativity.
The rewriting morphisms come first. Together with wf_exp_eq_morphism_iff1 and wf_exp_eq_morphism_iff2 from Definitions they let rewrite replace a term by an equal one anywhere in a judgment, including in the type.
Equations such as (Π A B)[σ] = Π A[σ] B[q σ] are not rules but equalities of exp, proved in Core.Syntactic.Substitution and used by rewrite or simpl_sub, so they need no optimized form. Neither does context equality, which is Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Δ ⊢ˢ Id : Γ in both directions.

    with signature wf_exp_eq Θ Ξ Γ Typeω@i ==> eq ==> iff as wf_exp_morphism_iff3.
Proof.
  intros A A' HAA M; split; intros HM.
  - eapply wf_conv; [ exact HM | exact (presup_exp_eq_right HAA) | exact HAA ].

Qed.

Corollary wf_prop_small : forall Θ Ξ Γ o,
    ⊢ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ->
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ Propₘ : Type⟨𝕃ᵒ o⟩.
Proof. intros; eapply (lift_exp_universe_index _ _ _ _ (us ordinal_zero) (us o)); [ cbn; ordinal_solve | apply wf_prop; assumption ]. Qed.

  intros * ? Hlt; destruct o as [a b].

The parts of a Π at the kinds κd κc: the premises wf_fn and wf_app ask of its domain and codomain.
#[warnings="-cannot-define-projection"]
Record pi_parts (Θ : gdeps) (Ξ : gstack) (Γ : ctx) (κd κc : abs_kind) (A B : typ) : Prop := mk_pi_parts
  {
The sort of the domain
    pi_parts_domain_sort : exp
    
The level of the universe the codomain's sort is next to
  ; pi_parts_codomain_level : nat
    
The domain's sort is a sort of its kind
  ; pi_parts_domain_sort_kind : sort_kind pi_parts_domain_sort κd
    
The domain is of it
  ; pi_parts_domain : Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ A : pi_parts_domain_sort
    
The codomain is of the sort of its kind
  ; pi_parts_codomain : Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▹{κd} A ⊢ B : abs_kind_sort κc Typeω@pi_parts_codomain_level
    
The kinds are those of a Π
  ; pi_parts_kinds : pi_kinds κd κc }.

The parts of a Π at the kinds κd κc typed at anything. Whenever a Π-type is the type of a term this recovers the premises the elimination and introduction rules want.
Lemma wf_pi_parts_at : forall Θ Ξ Γ κd κc A B C,
    Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ⊢ a_pi κd κc A B : C ->
    pi_parts Θ Ξ Γ κd κc A B.
Proof.
  intros * H; destruct (wf_pi_inversion_at H) as [U HU HA HB Hk _].
  destruct (pi_domain_eq_sort HU HA HA ltac:(apply wf_exp_eq_refl; exact HA)) as (S & HS & HA' & _ & _).
  enough (exists i, Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▹{κd} A ⊢ B : abs_kind_sort κc Typeω@i) as [i HBi]
    by exact (mk_pi_parts _ _ _ _ _ _ _ S i HS HA' HBi Hk).
  assert (HΓA : ⊢ Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▹{κd} A) by (eapply wf_ctx_extend; eassumption).
  rewrite abs_kind_sort_wk in HB.
  destruct κc; cbn [abs_kind_sort] in HB |- *; [| exists 0; exact HB | exists 0; exact HB ].
  inversion HU; subst; cbn [exp_wk] in HB; [ eexists; eassumption |].
  assert (Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▹{κd} A ⊢ʷ ↑ : Γ) by mauto 2.
  match goal with HL : _ ⍮ _ ⍮ _ ⊢ ?L : Level@?n |- _ =>
    assert (Θ ⍮ Ξ ⍮ Γ ▹{κd} A ⊢ L[↑]ʷ : Level@n) by (eapply (wk_preserves_exp _ _ _ _ (Level@n)); eassumption) end.
  exists 0; eapply wf_exp_suniv_large; eassumption.
Qed.

A variable has one type in a context; the variable case of the soundness proof uses it.

Lemma ctx_lookup_functional : forall n T Γ,
    Γ ∋ #n : T ->
    forall T',
      Γ ∋ #n : T' ->
      T = T'.
Proof.
  induction 1; intros; progressive_inversion; eauto.

Qed.
