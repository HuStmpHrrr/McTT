Mctt.Core.Completeness.Inductives.ElimViewRule

The Eliminator at a View, as Its Container's

The semantic rule E1′: the eliminator of a valid group at its view m of the container's member k' is the container's eliminator at k' with the wrapped blocks (view_wrap). At a semantic substitution of values C1 and C2, the chain of four values links through the group's eliminator at C2: the group's eliminator against itself (elim_stage), and the container's at C2 and at the moved blocks against the group's at C2 (cross_elim_all, with its stuck eliminators read alike by cross_bot_all).
