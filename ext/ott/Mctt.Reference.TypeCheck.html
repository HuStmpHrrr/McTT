The Reference Checker

The direct implementation of the algorithmic judgments, rule by rule: an application normalizes the instantiated codomain B[Id,,N] of the head's type, and a check normalizes both types it compares (subtyping_env_impl). Every normalization is at the initial environment of its context, which the checker carries (checker_env) and extends under binders. It is not extracted: Extraction.TypeCheck is the checker that is, checking an application spine against the value of the head's type, and Reference.Refinement proves the two accept the same programs, at types equal up to proofs. The parts they share (orders, decisions of side conditions, the initial environment, eliminators and records) are in Extraction.TypeCheckBase, Extraction.IndCheck and Extraction.RecordCheck.
