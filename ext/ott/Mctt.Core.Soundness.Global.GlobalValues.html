Values of Global Modules, Glued

The members of a stored body, read bare at the target of a sound embedding (glu_gctx_embedding), are glued at their stored types (glu_bare_member_at): a member with a δ-reduct as the reduct, which the embedding glues (a stored body, a type former of a group or of a record type), and one without as the filed axiom or abstract definition it is (mem_alias_filed). The values of global modules are glued from them (glu_dmod).
