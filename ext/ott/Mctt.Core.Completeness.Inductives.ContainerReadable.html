The Container's Eliminator's Entries Read Back

The container's eliminator at a view's wrapped blocks (view_wrap) stores the variables of its motives and branches at the container's types at its uniform parameters ub: the non-uniform parameters at the container's telescope, the arguments at the container's constructor's, the recursive results at the container's motives' instances. The elements it takes them from are the group's at the view (ElimView.CrossElim): related at the group's canonical relations. They read back at the container's types: the group's elements at a view are the container's at the view's instance cups (ViewCoherence.view_group_to_container), and so at any uniform parameters related to it, as ub is; the container's own uniform parameters at ub then read them back (elim_ups_ok).
These are ElimView.CrossElim's and ViewReadback.CrossBot's hypotheses Hcmot_x and Hcbr_x, which ElimViewRule discharges here.

The Domains of a Container's Shape in the Group's Relation

container_doms_link dr k drQ kQ pre Ts preQ TsQ t' t: the domains of the container's shape t', after the prefix preQ of the telescope TsQ in the container's relation drQ, against those of the group's shape t after pre of Ts in the group's dr: an element of the container's domain is one of the group's, and the rests after it. container_doms_link nests through a conjunction; its nested induction principle needs the All scheme of and.
