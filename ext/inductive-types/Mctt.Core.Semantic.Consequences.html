The Parameters of an Inductive Type

The normal form of an inductive type is the type former at the normal forms of its parameters, each equal to its parameter; so equal inductive types have equal parameters.
Lemma nbe_ty_ind_params : forall Γ p D Ms W, ind_lookup gc_deps gc_stack p = Some D -> ⊢ Γ ->
    List.length Ms = ind_np D ->
    (forall k P T, List.nth_error Ms k = Some P -> List.nth_error (tele_insts (ind_params D) Id Ms) k = Some T -> Γ ⊢ P : T) ->
    nbe_ty_f Γ (apps (a_ind p) Ms) W ->
    exists Rs, W = nf_indty p Rs /\ exps_eq_at Γ (ind_params D) Ms (nfs_to_exps Rs).
Proof.
  intros * Hl HΓ HMl HMt HW.
  destruct (decl_of_lookup _ _ _ _ _ HΓ Hl) as [Hdt Hc].
  pose proof (decl_closed_params D Hc) as Hass.
  pose proof (dc_params _ Hc) as Hs0.
  pose proof (dt_params _ _ _ Hdt) as HΔ.
  inversion HW as [ρ a Hinit Ea HRa]; subst.
  apply Modules.eval_apps_exp in Ea as (f & ms & Ef & Ems & Eap).
  inversion Ef; subst.
  apply eval_apps_ind_inv in Eap; cbn in Eap; subst.
  inversion HRa; subst.
  match goal with H' : ind_lookup _ _ p = Some ?D0 |- _ => assert (D0 = D) as -> by congruence end.
  eexists; split; [ reflexivity |].
  destruct (soundness_fundamental_ctx _ HΓ) as [Sb HSb].
  pose proof (initial_env_glu_rel_exp Hinit HSb) as Hσ.
  pose proof (completeness_fundamental_ctx _ HΓ) as HΓs; destruct (sem_ctx_per_ctx_env HΓs) as [RΓ HRΓ].
  pose proof (IndElimCases.params_glu_args _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ Hass Hs0 (completeness_fundamental_ctx _ HΔ) HΓs HRΓ HSb Hσ
                HMl
                ltac:(intros; eapply completeness_fundamental_exp; eauto)
                ltac:(intros; eapply soundness_fundamental_exp; eauto) Ems) as Hg.
  rewrite (List.map_ext (fun M => M[Id]) (fun M => M) exp_sub_id), List.map_id in Hg.
  destruct (IndCases.glu_args_params_top _ _ _ _ Hass Hs0 Hg) as [_ _ Htop].
  pose proof (Htop Γ wk_id _ ltac:(mauto 3) ltac:(eassumption)) as H.
  rewrite map_wk_id in H; exact H.
Qed.

Corollary exp_eq_ind_apps_inv : forall Γ p D X Y i, ind_lookup gc_deps gc_stack p = Some D ->
    Γ ⊢ apps (a_ind p) X ≈ apps (a_ind p) Y : Typeω@i ->
    List.length X = ind_np D -> List.length Y = ind_np D ->
    (forall k P T, List.nth_error X k = Some P -> List.nth_error (tele_insts (ind_params D) Id X) k = Some T -> Γ ⊢ P : T) ->
    (forall k P T, List.nth_error Y k = Some P -> List.nth_error (tele_insts (ind_params D) Id Y) k = Some T -> Γ ⊢ P : T) ->
    exps_eq_at Γ (ind_params D) X Y.
Proof.
  intros * Hl H HXl HYl HXt HYt.
  assert (HΓ : ⊢ Γ) by (gen_presups; assumption).
  destruct (completeness_ty H) as (W & HWX & HWY).
  destruct (nbe_ty_ind_params _ _ _ _ _ Hl HΓ HXl HXt HWX) as (Rs & -> & HX).
  destruct (nbe_ty_ind_params _ _ _ _ _ Hl HΓ HYl HYt HWY) as (Rs' & E & HY).
  injection E as <-.
  destruct HX as [L1 H1]; destruct HY as [L2 H2]; split; [ congruence |].
  eapply (params_eq_trans _ p D X (nfs_to_exps Rs) Y Hl HΓ); [ exact HXl | congruence | congruence | exact H1 |].
  eapply (params_eq_sym _ p D Y (nfs_to_exps Rs) Hl HΓ); [ exact HYl | congruence | exact H2 ].
Qed.

Subtyping of Universe Terms and Level Types

The three universe rules and the rule for the types of levels, as a relation on universe terms and level types: large below large by the order on levels, small below small by the order on level terms, small below large, and Level@m below Level@n by the order on sorts.
Inductive univ_sub (Γ : ctx) : exp -> exp -> Prop :=
| usub_large : forall i j, i <= j -> univ_sub Γ Typeω@i Typeω@j
| usub_small : forall t t' n,
    Γ ⊢ t : Level@n ->
    Γ ⊢ t' : Level@n ->
    Γ ⊢ maxl t t' ≈ t' : Level@n ->
    univ_sub Γ Type⟨t⟩ Type⟨t'⟩
| usub_small_large : forall t j n,
    Γ ⊢ t : Level@n ->
    univ_sub Γ Type⟨t⟩ Typeω@j
| usub_level : forall m n, m <= n -> univ_sub Γ Level@m Level@n.

The Normal Forms of Small Types

A small type's normal form is a type constructor of the small tier: ⊤, ⊥, Level, a Π, or a small universe at a literal level below the one the type lives in. It is never a large universe (the large tier is not below the small one), and never a small universe at a level with an atom: such a universe is in no small universe at a literal level, since an atom is unbounded under some assignment.

Opaque Definitions

With abstract definitions allowed, a closed normal form of a type shaped as the natural numbers can be stuck: an opaque global applied to arguments, as c is in abstract c : Nat := 3. What survives is:
  • at the global context itself, every closed normal form of such a type is a chain of its constructors, or one wrapped around a neutral headed by a global that does not unfold (canonical_form_of_ind_nat_stuck), with no hypothesis at all;
  • without axioms that global is an opaque definition with a body, and after unsealing every definition the term computes to a chain, equal in the unsealed context to the sealed normal form (canonical_form_of_ind_nat_no_axioms);
  • without axioms there is no closed proof of ⊥ (consistency_False_no_axioms): unsealing preserves typing (unseal_exp) and produces a transparent context (gc_no_axioms_unseal_transparent).
