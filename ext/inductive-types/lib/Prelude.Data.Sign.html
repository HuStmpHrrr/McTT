Signs, after Agda's Data.Sign: negative and positive (Agda's - and +), with the opposite sign and the multiplication of signs, under which they form a group with unit positive. The laws are in Prelude›Data›Sign›Properties.

module Prelude›Data›Sign where

inductive Sign : Type@0 where
| negative
| positive
end

open Sign export (negative; positive)

def opposite (s : Sign) : Sign ≔
  elim s return z . Sign | negative ⇒ positive | positive ⇒ negative end
end

The sign of a product: mul positive t is t, mul negative t is opposite t.

def mul (s : Sign) (t : Sign) : Sign ≔
  elim s return z . Sign | negative ⇒ opposite t | positive ⇒ t end
end

end
