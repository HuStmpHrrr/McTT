Prelude›Builtin›Nat

The natural numbers, as an inductive type bound as the builtin natural numbers: once this unit is imported, directly or not, the numerals denote chains of its constructors, 2 is succ (succ zero), and the token ℕ denotes Nat. The unit exports the constructors, so a client imports them with the type: import Prelude›Builtin›Nat use (Nat; zero; succ).

module Prelude›Builtin›Nat where

inductive Nat : Type@0 where
| zero
| succ (n : Nat)
end

builtin natural ≔ Nat

open Nat export (zero; succ)

end
